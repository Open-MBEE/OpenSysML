- **A v1 connector that only carries item flows is migrated as its flows alone.** The SysML v1 migrator wrote a connector realizing item flows as a `connection` plus, beside it, an anonymous `flow of T from a.p.x to b.q.x` per item flow, two unrelated members a renderer draws as two lines where the source tool drew one connector with an item-flow label. A flow is itself the connection between the two ports, so a connector carrying nothing of its own — no name, type, comment or stereotype — whose every item flow is writable is now written as its flow(s) alone, each named after its item flow (an anonymous one a diagram shows is named after the connector's ends, as a shown connection is, and marked `MigrationMetadata::SynthesizedName`; a strict migration lists it in the stand-in comment instead), and the diagram's `DiagramLayout::Route` and the view's `expose` go on the flows, every flow of the connector exposed and routed. The report says the connector was written as its flows. A connector with a name or type of its own, a comment, a stereotype, or an item flow that cannot be written keeps its `connection` as before, and its writable flows are still named after their item flows (unexposed, since the view already exposes the connection). An interconnection view now reads a `Route` about a `flow` as it does one about a `connection`. OMG's `FlowUsage_Factory` spelling (`flow f :> c of T from … to …`) is not used: both this validator and the pilot's reject it, which the transformation census records.
