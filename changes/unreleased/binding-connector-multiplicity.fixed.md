- **A binding connector's own multiplicity now bounds the links it declares.** `binding [1] bind [0..*] base.edges = [0..*] be` links at most one value of each end, so an end whose feature must hold more — `be [2]` — is a partial binding rather than a whole-binding multiplicity violation. ShapeItems `Cylinder`/`Cone`/`Box` faces and edges now resolve through it (`cyl.base.edges`, `block.voids.base.edges`), and binding diagnostics quote the connector multiplicity (`binding [1] bind …`).
