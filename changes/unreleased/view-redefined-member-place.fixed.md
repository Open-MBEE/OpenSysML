- A tree or interconnection rendering lists a member that redefines an inherited one in the redefined member's place, after the inherited members its definition declares before it, instead of ahead of every inherited member: refining a part in a usage body (`part :>> instrumentUnit { attribute :>> mass = 2000 [kg]; }`) no longer moves it to the top, so the landing page's Saturn V variants keep the instrument unit in the same row. Redefinitions declared in another order than the members they redefine keep those members' order, the own members declared after a redefinition follow it, and a redefinition that masks nothing, such as a parameter's positional one, stays where the body declares it.
