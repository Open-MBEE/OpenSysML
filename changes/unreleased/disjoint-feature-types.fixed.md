- `redefinition-type-mismatch`, which warned whenever a redefining feature's declared type did not conform to the redefined feature's — on `class B;` (unrelated, a legal feature typed by both) and `class B disjoint from A;` (no values) alike — is withdrawn: a redefining feature's type joins the redefined feature's (KerML 1.1 §8.3.3.3.4) and neither the specification nor the pilot checks that the two conform. In its place `disjoint-feature-types` warns, on redefinition and subsetting alike, only when two of a feature's types are provably disjoint, so it can have no values: declared `disjoint from` (§7.3.2.5), inherited along their specializations, through a union of disjoint types, or the kernel library's `Occurrence disjoint from DataValue` (a class and a data type).
