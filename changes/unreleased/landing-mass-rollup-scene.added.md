- The homepage hero has a second scene, the Saturn V mass roll-up from `examples/runtime-showcase/mass-rollup.sysml`: a visit opens on one of the two scenes at random, tabs switch between them over one connection to the engine, and `?scene=mass` or `#scene=stack` forces one. The scene draws the vehicle's stages and engines as nested boxes with the `totalMass` the engine rolls up on each, Run instantiates and evaluates the vehicle and animates the roll-up from the engines up, Edit changes a mass and the totals follow, and "Add the instrument unit" switches to `saturnVWithIU`, whose roll-up the engine reports undetermined with its reason. A scene now declares its controls, presenter, program and variants; the landing model draws one box per instantiated object, folding a bound collection into the objects it lists.
