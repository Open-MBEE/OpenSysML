- **A terminate action usage's body may state a flow.** `action stop terminate { assign code := 42; then terminate; then assign other := 7; }` now runs its `then` chain, where it was refused as stating no flow of its own. The terminate the usage stands for is unordered against its body's statements: `explore` and `-engine check` reach it falling before, between or after them, an output pin left unwritten binding no value, while `reverse` and `declared` still perform it after the body. A `terminate` naming the usage inside its own body ends the usage's performance rather than the body's.
