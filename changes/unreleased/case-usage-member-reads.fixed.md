- Reading a member of a calc, analysis or verification case usage through the usage answers every feature the usage has, not only its outputs: the subject, an actor or an `in` parameter as the usage binds it (`p1.v.s`, `VerPkg::vp.s`, `holder.check.who.id`, `c1.x`), a local or part the body declares (`check.depth`, `check.gear.id`), and the objective and the other members read through the case's occurrence (`scaled.fits.result`) — on an instantiated owner and at package level alike — instead of `unknown output`. A subject, actor or input nothing binds reads `<undetermined>` whatever its lower bound (`in vals : Integer[0..*]` included; an unbound actor or input leaves the case running); return-parameter reads are unchanged. Actors are parameters of analysis and verification cases, bound by name as the subject is, and one bound to several values keeps the multiplicity of the actor it redefines. An actor a usage renames at its position (`actor crew = op` over the definition's `actor pilot`) binds the actor it redefines, so the body's reads of `pilot` see the value. A case usage declared on a part has one occurrence per owner object: two parts' `check.gear` are two objects, each with its owner's values, instead of one shared object.
