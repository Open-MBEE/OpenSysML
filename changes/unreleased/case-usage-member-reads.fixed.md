- Reading an analysis or verification case usage's subject, actor or `in` parameter through the usage (`p1.v.s`, `VerPkg::vp.s`, `holder.check.who.id`) answers the value the usage binds, on an instantiated owner and at package level alike, instead of `unknown output`; the objective and the other members the case declares read through the case's occurrence (`scaled.fits.result`); a subject nothing binds reads `<undetermined>`, and an actor nothing binds is undetermined while the case still runs. Actors are parameters of analysis and verification cases, bound by name as the subject is.
