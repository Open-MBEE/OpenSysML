- The documentation site, the README and the Jupyter kernelspec follow the [OpenSysML brand guidelines](https://www.openmbee.org/opensysml-brand.html). The README and the landing page show the official OpenSysML badge (light and dark versions), linked to opensysml.org. The site header, the favicon and the kernelspec's `logo-32x32.png` and `logo-64x64.png` show the OpenMBEE icon, because the badge is not used below 96 px. The site takes the brand palette (`#10052F`, `#64DDFA`, `#D6D6D6`, and `#3E8DB5` as the accent on light backgrounds) in place of Material's indigo and sets headings and body text in ABeeZee. The social preview image is regenerated from the landing page.
