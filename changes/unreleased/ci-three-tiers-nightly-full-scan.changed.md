- CI runs in three tiers (CONTRIBUTING.md, "GitHub Actions"): a pull-request push runs the quick tier as before; the merge queue runs the queue tier, every shard under `-race` but the `examples/` sweeps (`scripts/race-shard.sh --sweeps`, `make test-queue`), which run race-free over the construct-cover subset (`OPENSYSML_CORPUS_SUBSET=construct-cover`); the full tier — everything under `-race` over the whole corpus — runs on the label `ci:full`, on an unclaimed path, on `workflow_dispatch` of `pr.yml` and every night over `develop`'s head (`.github/workflows/full-scan.yml`, which skips a head that already has one). `scripts/ci-verified-tree.py` accepts a queue-tier run for CircleCI's `build-test` skip on `develop` and only a full-tier run (or a green `build-test` of `main`) for a tag; CircleCI's own race run, coverage and release workflow stay the full scan.
