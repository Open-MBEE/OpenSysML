- The whole module now builds with `-tags sysml_re` and `-tags sysml_nov1`: `go build ./...` failed in the service package, which called the SysML v1 migration those builds leave out. Such a build of the service no longer reports the `migrate` capability and refuses `Migrate` as unimplemented, naming the capability.
- The WebAssembly builds of `sysml-mcp` and `sysml-jupyter-kernel` refuse, with status 2 and the reason, what they could only appear to do: `sysml-mcp -http` and serving a kernel both bind addresses, and a WebAssembly build's network reaches only the process it runs in, so no client could connect. `sysml-mcp` over stdin/stdout is unaffected.
