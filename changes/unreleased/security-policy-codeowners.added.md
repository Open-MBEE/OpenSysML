- A security policy, `SECURITY.md`, says how to report a vulnerability privately, who responds,
  the acknowledgement, triage and fix deadlines (critical within 14 days, high and medium
  within 60), the coordinated-disclosure terms, how automated and AI-assisted reports are
  handled, which versions receive fixes, and how to verify a release's signed checksum
  manifest and provenance. `.github/CODEOWNERS` names the repository's owners.
