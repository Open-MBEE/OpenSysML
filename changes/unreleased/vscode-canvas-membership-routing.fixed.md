- The VS Code diagram panel routes an edge between an owner and a member drawn inside it — the membership edges of a mixed rendering, a tree's structural usages — inside the owner's box: from the owner's header line, straight above the member where the row allows, to the member's border, through the lanes between the other members and never outside the owner; an edge between two siblings stays inside their container, and an edge that leaves a container crosses its border below the header, not through the header. A container whose members are reached from its header spreads them a lane apart, with the first row a lane below the header, so the routes and their keywords (`require`, `assume`, …) have room. An edge's label now sits on the segment nearest the route's middle where it covers no node's header and no earlier label; an edge the router finds no room for inside its container is routed around the boxes as before.
