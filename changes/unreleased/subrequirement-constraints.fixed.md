- A requirement usage nested in a requirement definition or usage is a subrequirement (SysML v2 §7.21.2): one of the container's required constraints, its value its own `assumptions implies constraints`, bound to the container's subject when it declares no subject of its own and keeping one it declares, through any depth of nesting. A failing subrequirement now fails its container — `-requirement`, `result`, the gRPC `VerifyRequirement` verdict and the MCP `verify_requirement` tool — where the container was reported as holding vacuously; a container whose own subject nothing binds reports the unbound feature instead of passing.
