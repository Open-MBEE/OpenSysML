- A transition with an accepter whose source is an action usage of any kind but a state usage — a do or exit action, a performed, accept, send or terminate action, a calculation, a case, named directly, through a chain or by inheritance, or the action a transition written without a source follows — is now reported as `accepter-source-not-state` (`A transition with an accepter must have a state as its source.`) at the accepter, as SysML v2.1 §8.3.18.9 `validateTransitionUsageTriggerActions` states it and the pilot reports it; it was rejected as `not-a-vertex` or `transition-source-not-vertex` before, the accepter rule naming an entry action only. A `transition` in an action body with an accepter and an action as its source, which was accepted and its accepter ignored, is reported the same way.
