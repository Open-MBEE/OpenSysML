- **A decision's branch stating a trigger, or another source through `sysml:sourceFeature` alone, is refused.** `if g then b;` and `else b;` have no notation for a trigger and take their source from the member before them, yet a graph stating `sysx:trigger` on such a branch was written back without it, and one stating a different source through `sysml:sourceFeature` rather than `sysml:source` passed the source check and was rebound to the member before it. Both are now refused as unsupported, as a branch stating another `sysml:source`, an effect or a body already is.
