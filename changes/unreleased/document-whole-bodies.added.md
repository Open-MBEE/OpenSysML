- Whole-document markup bodies: a Markdown or AsciiDoc `rep` in a `Document` or `Section` may now carry headings (`#`…`######`, `==`…), which open `Section`s nested by level in place among the sibling parts, so one body can write a whole document outline. The first heading met is one level below the owner; a heading that skips a level or rises above the body's base is the new `document-plan-markup-heading-level` planning error, and a heading in a `part : Paragraph` body stays refused. Each such section is named by its AsciiDoc `[[id]]`/`[#id]`, else by a slug of its title (lower-cased, runs of other characters one underscore, `_2`, `_3`… on a repeat), and `[](sysml:<name>)`, `sysml:ref[<name>]`, `<<name>>` and `sysml:<Document>::<name>` from another document resolve to it; numbering, tables of contents and title pages treat it as any section.
