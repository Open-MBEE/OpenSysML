- Packages that publicly import one another in a cycle analyse in time polynomial in their number again (issue #633): since imported memberships are read with the namespaces being read excluded, every route through such a cycle was searched and enumerated on its own, so fifteen mutually importing packages took over half a minute and thirty did not finish. A name no namespace among the imports holds is now found absent in one walk over them, and what each import of a cycle brings is computed as a least fixpoint over the cycle, read route by route only where a namespace is read in two ways (with and without its private memberships, or recursively and not). Thirty such packages validate in under three seconds; resolution on acyclic imports does not change.
