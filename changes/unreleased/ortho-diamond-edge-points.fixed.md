- `-render-lines ortho` no longer pins a cluster's invisible anchor point on a node drawn in the cluster. The anchor sat at the centre of the cluster's box, and in a drawing the model places with `Layout` that is often where a merge or decision diamond is; Graphviz, which routes such a drawing, then answered "the bounding boxes of some nodes touch - falling back to straight line edges" and drew every edge straight. Under the orthogonal routing the anchor is now pinned at the nearest point of the cluster clear of its members, the pins and the notes.
