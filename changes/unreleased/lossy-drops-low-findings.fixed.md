- Several inputs that were silently dropped or misread are now refused or read as written. A compact `api-json` reference object holding a key beside its numeric `@id` is refused instead of losing that key. A relative IRI in Turtle is resolved against `@base` as RFC 3986 section 5 states (`../x`, `/x`, `#x`, the empty reference) instead of being appended to it. An argument that conforms to one parameter it is bound to is still judged against the next, so the non-conforming binding is warned about. An SMT solver whose executable is not named after `z3` or `cvc5` gets its family's arguments from its `--version` banner. `ActionExecutor.SetInputs` keeps its own copy of the inputs, `WithBaseModel` in the Go client names the model's hash as it was when the option was made, `symbols.Changes.Empty` no longer reports a change to the registered spellings as empty, and the JavaScript client refuses a `real` that is not a finite number with a `MalformedValueError` in both directions, pointing at `{ kind: "infinity" }` for the unbounded value.
