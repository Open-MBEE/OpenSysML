- Several inputs that were silently dropped or misread are now refused or read as written. A compact `api-json` reference object holding a key beside its numeric `@id` is refused instead of losing that key. A relative IRI in Turtle is resolved against `@base` as RFC 3986 section 5 states (`../x`, `/x`, `#x`, the empty reference) instead of being appended to it, with its characters kept as written. An argument that conforms to one parameter it is bound to is still judged against the next, so the non-conforming binding is warned about. `ActionExecutor.SetInputs` keeps its own copy of the inputs, `WithBaseModel` in the Go client names the model's hash as it was when the option was made, and `symbols.Changes.Empty` no longer reports a change to the registered spellings as empty.
