- `%configure`, `%solve` and `%check` (and the MCP `configure` tool) accept a part or definition — a variation family or one of its configurations — and solve over the conjunction of every assertion about its objects: owned and inherited asserted constraints, carried requirements and satisfactions, the collection `%validate` checks, with the values its own features declare fixed; a carried requirement applies only where its assumptions hold, a satisfaction's requirement reads the part `by` names, and the report names nested parts whose own assertions are not in the query. A refused selection names the assertion refusing it; a part asserting nothing about its objects is an error.
