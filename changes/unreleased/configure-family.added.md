- `%configure`, `%solve` and `%check` (and the MCP `configure` tool) accept a part or definition — a variation family or one of its configurations — and solve over the conjunction of every assertion about its objects: owned and inherited asserted constraints, carried requirements and satisfactions, the collection `%validate` checks, with the values its own features declare fixed. A refused selection names the assertion refusing it; a part asserting nothing about its objects is an error.
