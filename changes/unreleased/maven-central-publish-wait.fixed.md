- The release workflow's `publish-maven` job no longer goes red while Maven Central is still publishing an uploaded deployment. `central-publishing-maven-plugin` waits for the published state silently for up to 30 minutes, and CircleCI's default ten-minute no-output timeout killed the v0.11.0 wait about five minutes before the version landed on Central. The step now outlives the plugin's wait (`no_output_timeout: 35m` against an explicit `waitMaxTime` of 1800 s and `waitPollingInterval` of 5 s in `client/java/pom.xml`), a hygiene test holds the two values together, and `docs/project/releasing.md` says what a red Maven Central job means now that the plugin's own timeout decides it.
