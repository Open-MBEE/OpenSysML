- Resolving a name no longer lists every name a namespace owns to decide whether one of them hides an import: `HidesImportedName` and `ImportedBeside` ask the namespace's registrations directly and stop at the first match. Diagnostics do not change; `BenchmarkAnalyzeRequirementGroup` allocates 20–27% fewer bytes and takes 8–15% less time, `BenchmarkAnalyseResolved` allocates 23% fewer bytes and takes 7% less time, and loading or validating the stress model allocates 12–15% fewer bytes (`BenchmarkLoad`, `BenchmarkValidateSplit`).
