- An exact `WhereType` keeps an element under the metaclass reflection derives for it (`Model.MetaclassOf`) wherever the library states that metaclass, the kind's `@type` deciding only where it does not: a chain end's chain Feature (`connect a.o to b.i`) is a `Feature`, a conjugated port definition a `ConjugatedPortDefinition`, a transition a `TransitionUsage` — no longer kept beside the ends under `ReferenceUsage`, under `PortDefinition` or under `ActionUsage`. The chain Feature's `@type` reads `Feature`, as the pilot exports it.
