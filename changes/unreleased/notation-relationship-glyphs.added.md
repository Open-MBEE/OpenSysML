- The relationship glyphs of the SysML v2 graphical notation (§8.2.3) whose ends a rendering draws are now edges of their own kind in the DOT, PlantUML, Mermaid, D2 and text forms and the VS Code diagram panel: a `succession flow` (dashed, filled head, «succession flow») is told from a flow in action and interconnection renderings; a required, assumed or asserted constraint, a framed concern and an event occurrence are «require», «assume», «assert», «frame» and «event» edges with an open head; a timeslice or snapshot carries the three-quarter circle at its occurrence; a `::>` reference subsetting its hollow triangle and dots, labelled `references`; a package's `alias` of a drawn package the hollow circle of an unowned membership; and a view usage's `expose` statements dashed «expose» edges to the drawn elements, suffixed `::*`/`::**` as imports are. Where a grammar has no such shape the nearest one is written and the notation table in `docs/project/view-rendering-forms.md` records the difference.
