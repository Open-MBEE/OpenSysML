- REPL commands that take one argument refuse a line carrying more instead of running on the first word and dropping the rest: `%advance 2 min` advanced the clock by 2 and `%advance 1 bogus junk` by 1, and both now answer `error: invalid time …` as `sysml -advance "2 min"` does, while `%instantiate`, `%view`, `%graphs`, `%check`, `%explain`, `%solve`, `%break`, `%occurrence`, `%search`, `%save`, `%print` and `%verbosity` answer with their usage line, as `%validate` and `%metadata` already did. `%advance` still reads the seconds unit after the number, attached or apart: `30s`, `30 s`, `2.5 [s]`.
