- **The pull-request workflow runs on merge-queue commits.** `.github/workflows/pr.yml` triggers on `merge_group` as well as `pull_request`: a queued pull request's commit on `gh-readonly-queue/develop/...` gets the full tier over the merge group's own diff, keyed to a concurrency group of its own, and `Build and test` reports on it instead of leaving the entry to time out. A hygiene test holds the workflow to the trigger, the merge-group branches of `Changed areas` and the concurrency key.
