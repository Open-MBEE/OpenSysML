- **Add source-preserving metadata prefixes to existing declarations.** `add_metadata_prefix` resolves the metadata definition or its alias in the declaration's scope, detects duplicates by canonical identity, and requires both `authoring` and `metadata_prefix_authoring`. It refuses declarations without a grammar slot, places prefixes after declaration modifiers, and preserves all untouched source bytes. The parser enforces the SysML modifier order for action-body parameters, including that `ref` follows `derived`, `abstract` or `variation`, and `constant`.
