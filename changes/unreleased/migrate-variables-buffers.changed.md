- **The SysML v1 migrator now writes variables, variable actions, buffer nodes, ReduceAction and RaiseExceptionAction.** An activity's variables are declared as private features of its body (`private attribute` for data types, `private ref item` for other classifiers, `private ref` untyped), and the variable actions become assignments to them — a read binds its result pin, a clear assigns `null`, adds and removes use `SequenceFunctions::including`/`includingAt`/`excluding`/`excludingAt`. A CentralBufferNode leading to one target is mapped by the flows through it; a DataStoreNode stays approximated. A ReduceAction folds its collection by the reducer's v2 function (`collection->ControlFunctions::reduce {in x; in y; F(x, y)}`), and a RaiseExceptionAction is followed by a terminate action, since v2 has no exceptions.
