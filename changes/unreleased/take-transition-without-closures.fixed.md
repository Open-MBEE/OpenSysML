- **The execution runtime takes a state machine's transition without allocating.** Taking a transition put the executor under the choice points the guard stood on through two closures, one to release the control and one to put the executor back where it was, made again on every transition taken; a state machine looping on a transition ten thousand times made twenty thousand of them. The control is now released by its position and the executor restored from a value, so taking a transition allocates nothing of its own.
