- A requirement check is `allTrue(assumptions()) implies allTrue(constraints())` (`Requirements::RequirementConstraintCheck::result`), as the value read of `result`, the subrequirement check and the solver already judged it: a subject failing an `assume constraint` satisfies the requirement whatever its `require constraint`s read, instead of being reported as violating them. `%requirement`, `%satisfy`, `%validate`, `-satisfy`, `-requirement`, the gRPC verify RPCs and the analysis standing say why such a check held (`vacuous: assumption t.enabled of requirement levelOk did not hold, so it holds vacuously`), and `assert not requirement` / `assert not satisfy` of such a requirement fails naming the assumption, since the result it denies is true. An `assume constraint` of a plain constraint still decides nothing.
