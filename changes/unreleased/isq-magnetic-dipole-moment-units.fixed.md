- **The two `SI` magnetic dipole moment units are typed by the quantity they measure.** `ISQ::*` re-exports a `MagneticDipoleMomentUnit` from both `ISQElectromagnetism` (IEC 80000-6, L^3·M·T^-2·I^-1) and `ISQAtomicNuclear` (ISO 80000-10, L^2·I), and KerML 7.2.5.4 hides a name two imports bring, so the unqualified `MagneticDipoleMomentUnit` in `SI.sysml` names nothing by the letter and the electromagnetic unit by first match. The declared errata overlay now qualifies both lines in the bundled library: `'m²⋅A'` (line 233) by `ISQAtomicNuclear::MagneticDipoleMomentUnit`, so it no longer reports a dimension mismatch, and `'Wb⋅m'` (line 303) by `ISQElectromagnetism::MagneticDipoleMomentUnit`. The published library text is unchanged; the documentation records the eight `ISQ` names the clause hides and that resolution, like the OMG pilot's, binds the first import's member.
