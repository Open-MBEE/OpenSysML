- A mixed rendering draws the membership of every member nested in its owner's node as an edge from the owner to the member, with the glyph the graphical notation gives that membership: the feature membership's diamond for a usage — filled for a composite usage, hollow for a referential one, with the usage's multiplicity at its end — the owned membership's circle-plus for a nested package, definition or other member that is no feature, and the keyword glyph of a membership drawn as a relationship of its own (`require`, `assume`, `assert`, `frame`, `event`, a portion), so no kind is drawn twice and none is left without its glyph. The text form lists each membership under `relationships:` and PlantUML draws it from the container to the member inside it; DOT and Mermaid, which can only run such an edge from an invisible anchor inside the container, show the membership by the nesting alone. A `Route` about a nested usage now steers its membership edge, as in a tree, and the usage's typing takes a Route naming its definition; a positioned mixed drawing that leaves the usage unplaced folds both into the owner's labelled edge to the definition.
