- `Usage::mayTimeVary` (and so a usage's `isVariable`) is derived over the usage's effective `isComposite`: a performed action or exhibited state, referential by the notation (`perform`, `exhibit`), may vary in time as the specification derives (SysML v2.1 §8.3.6.4 `deriveUsageMayTimeVary`); the reflective read answered `false` for them. A `require`/`assume` constraint, the ConstraintUsage its member declares, derives the same way; it read `false`. A flow's declared payload (`of p : T`) is classified as the KerML `PayloadFeature` the notation names, not a `ReferenceUsage`, so it neither varies in time nor reads `isVariable`. An expression body's parameter (`{ in item : Real; ... }`), owned by the Expression, varies in time as the pilot derives it; it read `false`.
