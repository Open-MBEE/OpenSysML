- The Go client runs parameter sweeps: `Client.Sweep(ctx, model, symbolID, ranges, opts...)` puts an analysis case or calc through `RunSweep` and answers a `Sweep` — its rows in the service's order, the swept `Parameters`, `Sampled` and `Seed`, the `Instances` the rows refer to, the diagnostics and the `Standing` (engine, strength, bounds). A `SweepRange` steps a parameter from `Start` through `End` by `Step`; `SweepSamples` with `SweepSeed` draws the rows instead, and `SweepSubject`, `SweepArguments`, `SweepArgument` and `SweepEngine` bind what every row shares. The call is refused as `CodeUnimplemented` before it is sent when the service lacks `verification` (or `engines`, for a named engine), and a sweep the service refuses is a `*VerifyError`. `RunRow` now also carries each run's `Evaluations`, its `Elapsed` wall time and the `Reason` of a failed run, so the rows of `RunAction`, `RunMonteCarlo` and a recorded sweep report them too. `make conformance-pkg` now runs the shared `RunSweep` scenarios through the package instead of skipping them.
