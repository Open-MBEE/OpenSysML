- The temporal members of occurrences answer from what a run produced. `startShot`/`start` and `endShot`/`done` are the snapshots of an occurrence that began and ended, each an object of the occurrence's own type whose own shots are itself; `snapshots`, `timeSlices`, `portions` and the time-enclosed collections list the shots that happened and stay open, with the shots known, until the occurrence ends; a shot's `snapshotOf`, `timeSliceOf` and `portionOf` name its occurrence. `predecessors` and `successors` are the occurrences that ended before this one began and began after it ended, with their shots; `immediatePredecessors` and `immediateSuccessors` are the ends a succession fired between, one per fired order into a join. Every send is a `Transfers::Transfer` from the sending performance (else object) to the object it is bound for, begun when sent and completed by the accept that takes it, listed by `outgoingTransfers` and `incomingTransfers` of the performance, object and port at each end, with `source`, `target` and `payload`. `Clocks::TimeOf`/`DurationOf` read the shared clock at the start and end of an occurrence as the bare numbers the Kernel declares, `Time::TimeOf`/`DurationOf` as quantities of time; a shot or duration the run has not produced reads undetermined with the reason. A running-object golden (`TestKernelLibraryMembersReadableWhileRunning`) pins every Kernel Semantic Library member at points in seven runs, and snapshots, held images and traces carry the new state.
