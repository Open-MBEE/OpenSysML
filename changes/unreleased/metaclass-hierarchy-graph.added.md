- `sysml -metaclass-graph` writes the metaclass hierarchy of the pinned pilot metamodel as an RDF vocabulary graph in Turtle, without reading a model: one `rdfs:Class` per metaclass in the `sysml:` namespace, `rdfs:subClassOf` per declared general metaclass and `sysx:abstract true` on the abstract ones. The graph is committed as `internal/translate/rdf/ontology/sysml-metaclasses.ttl` with a drift gate (`make metaclass-graph-check`), so a store that holds one `rdf:type` per element can load it and find every specialization of a metaclass through `rdf:type/rdfs:subClassOf*`.
