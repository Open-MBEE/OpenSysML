- A printed condition keeps the grouping it was evaluated with: `a and (b or c)` is shown as `a and (b or c)`, not `a and b or c`, in `%validate`, `%constraint` and `%satisfy` verdicts, the conflict lines of `%check`, `%explain` and `%solve`, the `not <condition>` of a violated negation, the gRPC verdicts and the document query backends; so do the view's multiplicity bounds and a binding whose source text is unavailable. An operand is parenthesised only where its operator binds looser than the enclosing one, or as tight on the side the enclosing one does not associate to (`a - (b - c)`, `(a ** b) ** c`, `not (a and b)`, `-(a + b)`), so `(a and b) or c` still prints without them and every printed condition reads back as the tree that was checked.
