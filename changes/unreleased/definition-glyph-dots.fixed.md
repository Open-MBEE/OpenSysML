- The DOT form's typing edges are drawn with the notation's `definition` glyph: a hollow triangle with two small dots beside its base, one each side of the line. Graphviz's `onormaldotdot` set two full-size dots in a row along the edge instead, so a typing edge is now written `arrowhead=empty, class=definition` and the dots are drawn into the SVG wherever OpenSysML has Graphviz draw a view — Jupyter's `%viz`, rendered documents and the language server's drawings. DOT text drawn by another Graphviz shows the plain triangle.
