- **`check` no longer reports *exhaustive* where its moves leave a run out.** Under `check` a `do` behavior moves one token a step and the machine dispatches after each; under the fixed policies (`reverse`, `declared`, `seed:<n>`) the flow finishes its round first and the machine dispatches between rounds. A search reaching a machine that owes a dispatch after a `do` step that moved one of several tokens able to act therefore has no move making the fixed policies' run, and reported `no violation, exhaustive` over an enumeration short of it — a state whose `do` body loops through timed waits under a timed exit ended `right = 0` on every schedule searched where `reverse` ends it at `1`. Such a search now ends `no violation within bounds` (or `divergent`, when the schedules searched disagree) with `not enumerated: do round before dispatch` on the verdict line, the report's `NotEnumerated` (`-json`: `notEnumerated[]`) naming it, and the standing *bounded*; a search meeting no such state is *exhaustive* as before, and every witness replays as before. The ordering becomes a recorded choice point with the region-order scheduling work.
