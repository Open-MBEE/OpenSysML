- A merge group runs a client, editor or plugin CI job only when that client's own paths changed, as a pull request's quick tier already did; the fan-out from a service change to every client is the full tier's, which the nightly full scan dispatches on `develop`. Every client job fetches its toolchain and dependencies from a registry (PyPI, Maven Central, npm, crates.io, the Julia registry, ftp.gnu.org), and one registry timeout in a client the change never touched failed the whole group and cost its slot.
