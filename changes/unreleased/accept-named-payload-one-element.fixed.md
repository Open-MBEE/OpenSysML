- The payload a transition's trigger names (`accept x : Sig`) is one element again. It reflected as two symbols under one `elementId` — the declared `x`, an `ItemUsage` the transition owned, and an unnamed `ReferenceUsage` the trigger's accept owned — so `x.owningMembership` answered a membership whose `ownedMemberElement` was not `x`. The declared `x` is now the accept's `payloadParameter`: a `ReferenceUsage` owned by the accept through its `ParameterMembership`, no longer a member the transition owns, and reached by both its qualified name and the `@id` the tree writes for it.
