- A «Refine», «Satisfy», «Verify», «DeriveReqt», «Allocate» or plain dependency, or a connector, drawn on a SysML v1 activity, state machine, block definition or internal block diagram now keeps its line: the migrator routes it on the `ActionFlowView`, `StateTransitionView`, tree or interconnection view the diagram is written as whenever the view shows both of its ends (SysML v2 graphical notation 8.2.3.3 draws a dependency between any two shown element nodes), where it was reported `not drawn`; the action, state and tree renderings draw such exposed relationships as dashed open-arrow edges with their keyword, following their `Route`, and draw an exposed end that is no node of the graph as a plain box. A `verify requirement x :> R` or `satisfy requirement x :> R` member now ends its edge at `R`, not at the member itself; a requirement derivation written as a `connection def` is drawn as a `derive` edge.
