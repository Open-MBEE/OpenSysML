- Diagrams draw a port whose `DiagramLayout::Layout` is the source tool's rectangle — a SysML v1
  diagram sizes a port's shape to carry its name — as the notation's port square on its owner's
  border, named beside it: the Layout gives the port its side and place along it, from the
  rectangle's centre, never its size. The rule is one for a part's declared ports, the ports it
  has from its definition, and a definition's ports in a tree. An interconnection view that
  exposes a definition's port beside a part typed by the definition no longer draws the port
  twice, as the part's pin and as a box of its own over the part's title.
