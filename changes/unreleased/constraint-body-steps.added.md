- Statements in a constraint or requirement body (`assign`, `if`, `while`/`loop`/`for`, and body-local declarations interleaved with them) now run as steps of the check's own performance before its conditions are evaluated, in declaration order: locals live in a fresh frame per check, the constraint's own parameters are copied into it, and each condition of the body is judged in the state the steps left. Assignments reaching outside the performance — a name it holds no feature for, a chained or qualified target — and effects it cannot perform (`send`, `perform`, `terminate`) are refused with the typed `ErrConstraintExternalAssignment`/`ErrConstraintEffect`, as are stated successions (`ErrStatementNotExecutable`), and a body stating steps but no result expression reports `ErrNoConditions`. The solver refuses to translate the steps of such a body rather than solving its conditions as if they ran.
