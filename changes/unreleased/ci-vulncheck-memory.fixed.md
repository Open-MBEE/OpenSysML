- The CircleCI `Go static checks` job on `develop` is no longer killed at the `Check Go dependencies for known vulnerabilities` step. `make vulncheck` (govulncheck over the root and `tools/` modules) peaks at 3.9 GB and 4.1 GB, at the 4 GB ceiling of the `medium` class that job runs on, so some pipelines died with `signal: killed` before the scan printed anything. The step now runs as its own `Go vulnerability check` job on the 8 GB `large` class in every workflow that ran it, and the release workflow's publish jobs require it as they required the static checks.
