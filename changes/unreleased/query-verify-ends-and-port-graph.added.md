- **Queries answer the ends of a `verify` and the port a chained connector end reaches.** A `verify` in a verification case's objective reports `verifiedRequirement` (the requirement it references or declares) and `verifyingCase` (the case whose objective holds it; `sysx:verifyingCase` in OSLC query text), so the verifier half of a satisfy/verify consistency check is a predicate. A connector end written as a chain (`connect a.p to b.p`) reports its `sourceFeature`/`targetFeature` as written rather than omitting it, beside `sourceFeature.featureTarget` and `targetFeature.featureTarget`, the ports themselves.
