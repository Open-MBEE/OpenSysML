- A feature chain through a variant named with no selecting object — `Case::big.m` through the variation definition, `cases.big.m` or `engine.electric.power` through a variation usage, a variant with a declared value such as `TargetCases::largeCase.bodyMass`, or a part bound to a variant and read through — now evaluates to that variant's value, as the pilot does, instead of failing with `cannot chain through non-instance member (variant)`; a variant bound as an analysis or verification case's subject, directly or through a configuration part, is evaluated the same way. Selection semantics are unchanged: a bound variation still reads its one selected variant, and an unselected one still reports so.
