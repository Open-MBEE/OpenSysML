- **The local gate runner now honours cgroup limits inherited from parent cgroups and sizes each shard from the fleet actually in flight.** It walks the process's cgroup and its ancestors for the tightest CPU quota and smallest finite memory headroom, and divides the CPU budget over the shard subset and concurrency cap, so `-concurrency 4` no longer gives four shards the two-shard default's `GOMAXPROCS`.
- **A shard that skips its corpus is no longer reported as a clean pass.** `GATE NOT RUN` banners are counted from the full stream independently of the log tail and verbose output, and the summary marks those shards `WARN` and counts them as incomplete; a required corpus still fails its shard and exits non-zero.
