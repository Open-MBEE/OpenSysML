- A sequence rendering draws its successions in the order they are declared, whichever form states
  each: a member-attached `then` was drawn above the `succession` usages declared before it, so the
  last arrow of a chain appeared first. A chain declared out of order beside a branch is drawn first
  step first too: `succession first b then d; succession first a then c; succession first a then b;`
  drew `b then d` above `a then b`.
