- **The Rust client reads what the service could not send, and recovers from a dead private service.** A value the service sends as a null naming a reason is now `Error::UnsupportedValue` rather than `Value::Null`, and an instance feature holding one reports the reason through `FeatureValue::error` while the rest of the instance stays readable. `Connection::private` replaces a private service that has exited instead of handing out its dead address. Inline SysML content parses on a service without `inline_language`; `instantiate` refuses with `MissingCapability` on a service without `feature_values` before sending, as the instances it would return need; an empty vector is sent rather than refused; and `Symbol::children` leaves out a child the service cannot resolve instead of failing.
