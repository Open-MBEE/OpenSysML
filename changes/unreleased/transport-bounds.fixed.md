- The transports bound what a peer can hold open: `sysml-mcp` closes a Streamable HTTP session idle for 30 minutes; `sysml-grpc -stdio` handles at most 64 calls at once (a further call is answered `RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED`) and stops, rather than hanging, when the peer takes more than a minute to accept 4 KiB of an answer; and both stdio frame readers refuse a frame whose header lines exceed 64 KiB before buffering them.
