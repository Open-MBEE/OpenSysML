- **A `GeneralView` draws the requirement, definition and usage, and package graphs its filters select.** A GeneralView whose element filters name the metaclasses the OMG library lists for a specialization — `filter @SysML::RequirementUsage;`, `expose P::**[@SysML::Definition or @SysML::Usage];`, `filter @SysML::Package;`, or an `or` of them — is drawn as a graph of nodes and edges in `text`, `mermaid`, `dot` and `plantuml`, under the Pilot and Cameo styles and the palettes: requirements with their id and text and the satisfy, verify, derive, refine, allocate, specialization and typing edges between them; definitions and usages with specialization, typing, composition and reference edges; packages with containment and imports. An unfiltered GeneralView, and one whose filters are of any other shape, stays the containment tree it was. An opt-in `verdicts` overlay — `-render-overlay verdicts`, `%render <view> <form> verdicts`, `"overlay": "verdicts"` on `opensysml/render`, a document `Diagram`'s `overlay` — runs the verification cases verifying each drawn requirement and labels and colours it by their verdicts. A source link template (`-render-link`, `link=<template>`, `linkTemplate`) links the graphs' nodes and edges to their declarations as it does every other kind's. A GeneralView filtered on a case metaclass — `filter @SysML::UseCaseUsage;`, `@SysML::CaseDefinition`, or any metaclass specializing `CaseDefinition` or `CaseUsage` — is drawn as the case diagram, by the case writer, needing only the standard library: its every form, source links included, is what a `CaseView` exposing the same elements writes, but for the provenance line. `CaseView` is the shorter spelling, with the OpenSysML library; a mixed diagram still needs `MixedView`. When filters select more than one graph, requirement wins over package, package over case and case over definition and usage. See `examples/general-views-demo`.
