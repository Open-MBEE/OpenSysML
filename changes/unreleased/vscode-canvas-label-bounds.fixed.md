- The VS Code diagram panel's canvas now covers everything it draws: an edge label or arrowhead routed above, left or right of the outermost boxes — `«derive»` and `«satisfy»` over the top row of a requirement view, say — is no longer cut off at the canvas's edge. The canvas bounds are computed from the node boxes with their ports and labels, every edge polyline, the arrowheads at its ends and its label box, plus the margin; a dragged node grows the canvas by the same measure.
