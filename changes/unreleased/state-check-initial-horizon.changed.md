- `-engine check` on a `-state` without `-advance` now observes the machine after its initial transition only, the point the default run and `-schedule explore` observe, instead of running its do behaviors and advancing its clock to every wait; `-advance <time>` advances all three to the same horizon. When the machine leaves a do behavior, an event, a timer or a change condition pending there, the standing line of `explore` and `check` says the result is `after the initial transition only` and that `-advance <time>` runs them (`scope` in `-json`).
