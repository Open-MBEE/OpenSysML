- **An action definition that specializes another now performs the inherited steps, successions, flows and statements.** Previously `action def P :> B` silently ran only its own members, so `action def Plain :> Base;` completed with no results and `action def Extra :> Base { action b { ... } }` ran `b` but not `Base::a`. Inherited content is lowered into the specialization's flow (nearest general first, a diamond's shared general once), a step redefined by name replaces the inherited one and inherited successions retarget to it, and cyclic specialization, a redefinition naming a missing step, an incompatible replacement and an ambiguous retarget are reported as errors, and a specialization's own flows may connect inherited steps.
- **A specialization preserves gates on inherited succession flows.** A guarded succession targeting an inherited succession flow retains its guard and delivery, so the specialized action follows the same guarded path as its general.
- **A redefining action step with no multiplicity of its own now repeats as often as the step it redefines.** `action :>> a { … }` over `action a[3]` performs three times (it performed once), and `run`, `explore`, `check`, `validate` and the SMT refusal all use the same effective count; a redefining step also keeps the redefined step's body alongside its own.
- **An inherited assertion stays unchecked unless a succession or flow connection in its declaring body or elsewhere in the specializing action's inheritance chain names it.** A specialization therefore carries an assertion ordered by an intermediate general while leaving an unsequenced assertion unchecked.
- **A typed action usage, entry/do/exit behavior or performed action that states a body of its own now merges that body with the typed or performed action.** Its own members run alongside the action's inherited content as one flow, unordered unless a succession orders them, and its redefinitions replace inherited steps; an entry/do/exit behavior doing this is no longer reported at execution. A body that only binds pins still performs the action as before. An executable typed body nested in its own type, directly or through inheritance, is refused at execution rather than recursively unfolded; feature-only self-typed bodies remain lazy.
