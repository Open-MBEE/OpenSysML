- **An action definition that specializes another now performs the inherited steps, successions, flows and statements.** Previously `action def P :> B` silently ran only its own members, so `P0 :> B1` completed with no results and `P3 :> B1 { action b {…} }` ran `b` but not `B1::a`. Inherited content is lowered into the specialization's flow (nearest general first, a diamond's shared general once), a step redefined by name replaces the inherited one and inherited successions retarget to it, and cyclic specialization, a redefinition naming a missing step, an incompatible replacement and an ambiguous retarget are reported as errors.
- **A redefining action step with no multiplicity of its own now repeats as often as the step it redefines.** `action :>> a { … }` over `action a[3]` performs three times (it performed once), and `run`, `explore`, `check`, `validate` and the SMT refusal all use the same effective count; a redefining step also keeps the redefined step's body alongside its own.
- **A typed action usage, entry/do/exit behavior or performed action that states a body of its own now merges that body with the typed or performed action.** Its own members run alongside the action's inherited content as one flow, unordered unless a succession orders them, and its redefinitions replace inherited steps; an entry/do/exit behavior doing this is no longer reported at execution. A body that only binds pins still performs the action as before.
