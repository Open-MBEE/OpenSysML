- The reflective read of an invocation or constructor expression (`F(1, 2)`, `F(a = 1)`, `x->F(3)`, `new Pt(1, 2)`) now owns one `in` parameter per argument — the arrow form's receiver first, then positional, then named arguments — each held by a `ParameterMembership` and binding its argument through a `FeatureValue`, with the `out` result, as the RDF conversion already wrote them; `argument`, `parameter`, `input`, `ownedMembership` and `ownedElement` answer them and their `elementId`s are the ones the conversion writes.
