- **The Node/TypeScript client covers the whole service surface.** `@openmbee/opensysml` grew from the five v1 RPCs to everything the Python client does: `parseSources` for a model of several documents, `convert`/`save`, `query` and the document calls, `executeAction`/`executeState` and the `explore*` forms, `verifyConstraint`/`verifyRequirement`/`verifySatisfaction`/`validateInstance`/`calc`/`runAnalysis`/`runSweep` with `listEngines`, `model.edit()`/`connection.applyEdits` for source-preserving edits with the typed `EditError` family, `version`/`requireCapabilities` on `connect()` raising `StaleServiceError`, and the `opensysml-generate` CLI for typed view modules. `connection.rpc` remains the escape hatch for anything not wrapped.
