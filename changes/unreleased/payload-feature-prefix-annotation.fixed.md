- **Fix the flow/message payload and the dependency `#` prefix metaclasses in RDF and API-JSON export** — a flow's `of` payload is now a `PayloadFeature` the flow owns through a `FeatureMembership` ahead of its end members (a declared `of name : Type` payload is the declaration element itself), instead of a `sysx:payload` `FeatureReferenceExpression`; and a `#` metadata prefix ahead of a `dependency` is owned through an `Annotation` rather than an `OwningMembership` (SysML.xtext `PayloadFeatureMember`, `PrefixMetadataAnnotation`). Older graphs stating `sysx:payload` or the membership-owned prefix are still read.
