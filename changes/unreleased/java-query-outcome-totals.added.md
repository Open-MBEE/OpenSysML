- The Java client's `QueryOutcome` carries the totals behind the lists the service bounds: `QueryOutcome.Frontier` gains `membersTotal()`, `featuresTotal()` and `kindsTotal()`, and `QueryOutcome` gains `failuresTotal()`, `findingsTotal()` and `pathFailuresTotal()`, each counting every entry there was while its list holds at most eight, so a caller can say how many candidates or failures were left out. The components are additive: the constructors without totals remain and count their lists whole, as does a record decoded from a service that sends none (`docs/reference/java-api.md` § Document queries and rendering).
