- The solver judges a requirement that assumes but requires nothing as the evaluator does: its assumptions are the antecedent of its claim (`allTrue(assumptions()) implies allTrue(constraints())`), never asserted on their own, so `%check` and `%solve` of such a requirement — or of a satisfaction of one — are satisfiable instead of asserting the assumption, and the `holds` question of the gRPC `VerifyRequirement` and `VerifySatisfy` calls proves it from a violation query that is false instead of refusing with `ErrNoConditions`. A constraint assuming but requiring nothing still yields no verdict, as a `ConstraintCheck` states no result.
