- **A requirement's assume and require constraints are the members of its `assumptions` and `constraints` collections, each a constraint check with a `result`.** A constraint usage subsetting a constraint-typed collection (`assume constraint fullTank :> assumptions { … }`, Requirements.sysml `RequirementCheck::assumptions`) contributes its own object to it, inherited ones included, and that object's `result` is the usage's condition evaluated on the requirement — true, false, undetermined where an input is unbound, an error where the evaluation fails — instead of a featureless placeholder whose `result` was undetermined. Invoking such a collection (`allTrue(assumptions())`, the library's own `RequirementConstraintCheck::result`) is each member's result rather than the empty body of the library declaration; invoking a constraint member through a chain (`req.assumptions()`, `req.fullTank()`) and an assume or require constraint by name (`fullTank()`) applies it on its object; a constraint usage the object enacts reads as its verdict under its bare name as it did through a chain. The requirement's own `result` (`allTrue(assumptions()) implies allTrue(constraints())`) and its vacuity are unchanged. (#1854)
