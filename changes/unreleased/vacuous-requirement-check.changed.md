- **A requirement with no `require constraint` is satisfied, not unevaluable.** A requirement check's result is `allTrue(assumptions()) implies allTrue(constraints())` (SysML v2 §9.2.11 `Requirements::RequirementConstraintCheck`), and `allTrue` over an empty sequence is true (KerML §9.2.3 `ControlFunctions::allTrue`); a viewpoint or concern is such a check (§9.2.17 `Views::ViewpointCheck :> RequirementCheck`). `-satisfy`, `%satisfy`, `-requirement`, `%requirement`, `%view` and the gRPC checks now report a `satisfy` of a requirement, concern or viewpoint that states no required constraint as `✓ … holds`, with the standing naming why (`vacuous: viewpoint reading states no required constraint, so it holds vacuously`), where they reported `? … could not be evaluated … no condition to evaluate` and exited 2; `assert not satisfy` of such a requirement is violated, and `%check` of it is satisfiable by every assignment, while `%check` of a part asserting `not satisfy` of it is unsatisfiable. A constraint stating no condition still has no verdict, a denial or violation query of a condition-less requirement still has nothing to deny, and a `require r` naming the requirement being checked is now reported as the cycle it is instead of being read as no condition.
