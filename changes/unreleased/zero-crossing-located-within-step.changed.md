- A state-space `ZeroCrossing` is now located inside the step instead of at the end of the step that detected it. Before each step the runner previews every guard at the quarters of the step, over the state the integrator reaches from the step's start, keeping no effect of the preview; a sign change between two previews, or a guard that turns towards zero and is found to reach it, is bisected to two adjacent floating-point instants and the run takes a sample there. The event carries that instant (the trace prints it in full, `event: zero crossing touchdown of fall (t=1.949358868961793)`, where it read `t=2.0`), the state, `time` and `output` a state machine reads are those at the crossing, a `terminal` crossing ends the run there, and a guard that crosses and crosses back inside one step raises both events. An event occurrence typed by `ZeroCrossingEventDef` with no guard is located the same way over the components of the derivative. A run with crossings therefore reports more samples, and a terminal crossing a final state at the crossing rather than one step past it. A guard that crosses and recrosses between two previews without turning towards zero at one is still not seen, so the compliance row stays approximate.
