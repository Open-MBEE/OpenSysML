- `llm-bench` (tools/cmd/llm-bench) benchmarks how well a language model writes SysML v2 with the compiler in the loop: 50 natural-language tasks with gold models that validate under `sysml -validate` and the pinned pilot, a generate–validate–repair loop that feeds `sysml -validate -json` diagnostics back for up to three rounds, a compiler-only arm and an arm offering the `sysml-mcp` tools in-process, OpenAI-compatible, Anthropic and replay providers, and metrics for compile rate, repair rounds, structural fidelity over the API JSON export, unresolved-name hallucinations and pilot agreement; the method, corpus and results are recorded in docs/project/llm-benchmark.md.
