- The `drawio` rendering form no longer falls back silently when the router finds no path for an edge: the file carries a notice naming the edge and its cells, and an end at a port still leaves through the port's face. Two causes of such edges are gone: a flush pin at a quarter, half or three quarters of a side (a container's header pin above a centred member) coincided with one of libavoid's generic pins and attached to neither, in the diagram panel's router as well as in the export, and a member drawn outside its container was routed inside the container's bounds. Routed waypoints and anchors are written to a thousandth of a pixel instead of with float noise, while a stated `Route` keeps its exact points, and an edge that is a declaration of its own (a connection, flow, succession, transition, dependency and the like) carries its qualified name as `fqn` on its `<object>`, as the nodes do.
