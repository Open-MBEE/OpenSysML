- The `drawio` rendering form no longer falls back silently when the router finds no path for an edge: the file carries a notice naming the edge, and an end at a port still leaves through the port's face. Routed waypoints and anchors are written to a thousandth of a pixel instead of with float noise, and an edge that is a declaration of its own (a connection, flow, succession, transition, dependency and the like) carries its qualified name as `fqn` on its `<object>`, as the nodes do.
