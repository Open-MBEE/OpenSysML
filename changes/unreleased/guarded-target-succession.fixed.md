- **A decision's branches are `TransitionUsage`s.** `if g then b;` and `else b;` after a `decide` in an action body were written as `SuccessionAsUsage`s carrying their guard only as `sysx:guard` text; they are now the `TransitionUsage`s the grammar builds (SysML.xtext `GuardedTargetSuccession`, `DefaultTargetSuccession`), the guard owned through a `TransitionFeatureMembership` of kind `guard`, the `then` succession owned as a transition's is, and the decision as `source`. Both convert back to the notation they were written in.
