- The SysML v1 migration writes the lines a use case diagram draws as connections a tool can show: each actor association, «Include», Extend and «Refine» becomes a `connection` between `part`, `use case` or `requirement` usages of its ends, written once per definition in its own package only when a connection joins it, hosted by the nearest package holding both ends; a named association's one usage is typed by its `connection def` and named like a usage. A «Refine» keeps its other metadata in the connection's body and the refine as a comment, reported as approximated. Use case diagrams are rendered `asInterconnectionDiagram` over those usages and connections instead of `asTextualNotation`, with their Cameo geometry applied; the interconnection renderer draws use case and requirement usages as nodes.
