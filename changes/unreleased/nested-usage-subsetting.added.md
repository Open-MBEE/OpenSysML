- Nested usages implicitly subset the collection of their owner their kind nests under (SysML v2 §8.3): a `part` in a part is one of its `subparts`, an `item` one of its `subitems`, a `port` one of its `ownedPorts`, an `action` one of its `subactions`, a `state` one of its `substates`, and so on for calculations, constraints, requirements, cases, views and renderings — so `subparts->size()` counts the nested parts, the interfaces included, without spelling out `:> subparts`. The runtime applies this to model-declared usages; library-declared usages keep their declared subsettings.
- The `nested*` properties of `SysML::Usage` and the `owned*` properties of `SysML::Definition` (`nestedPart`, `nestedUsage`, `ownedPort`, …) are derived on reflective `meta` values, in declaration order.
