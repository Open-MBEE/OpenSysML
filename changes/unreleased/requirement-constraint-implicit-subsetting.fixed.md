- **A requirement's `assume` and `require` constraints now implicitly specialize `Requirements::RequirementCheck::assumptions` and `constraints`.** SysML v2 8.3.20.4 `checkConstraintUsageRequirementConstraintSpecialization` has a composite constraint owned through a RequirementConstraintMembership subset the collection its kind names, as the pilot derives it; the derivation stopped at the member's declaration form and named nothing, so the collections had no subsetters. The constraint member of a requirement definition or usage, written with `constraint` or in the reference form (`assume c1;`), now subsets the collection, which the runtime's subsetting queries see; a `ref` constraint is excluded as the rule excludes it.
