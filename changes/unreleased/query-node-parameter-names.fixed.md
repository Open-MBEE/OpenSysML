- `sysml -query` and gRPC `Query` read a parameter an action's notation states with no expression of its own by its role under its action: `parameter`, `input`, `feature` and `ownedFeature` of an assignment answer `P::A::assignCount::target` instead of an empty value, and a `for` loop answers its variable (`P::A::forAct::i`) instead of dropping the property. One stated by an expression reads as that expression, as a send's payload does (`new Sig()`): a `while` or `if` test, and the occurrence of `terminate sub;`, which read the library's `terminatedOccurrence` only while the action held no parameter of its own. `qualifiedName` of a `for` loop's variable is qualified by its loop for every reader of the semantic model, where it read the bare name.
