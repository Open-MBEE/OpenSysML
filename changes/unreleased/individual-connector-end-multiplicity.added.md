- An `individual` definition owns the empty `Multiplicity` its prefix declares (SysML.xtext `OccurrenceDefinitionPrefix`, `EmptyMultiplicityMember`): a nameless `Multiplicity` under a non-implied `OwningMembership`, first among its owned relationships, its `Type::multiplicity` — on reflection, RDF and API-JSON alike, identified as `<definition>_pmultiplicity`. A connector usage's named end (`connect e1 ::> a to e2 ::> b`, `l references x`) states its implicit `[1..1]` the same way, last among the end's owned relationships (`<end>_pmultiplicity`), as the pilot does for a declared end usage; an end with a written range states none. The verbatim-text check tolerates an older Turtle that omits them.
