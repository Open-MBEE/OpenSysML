- The semantic model holds the binding connector an accept action owns between its receiver parameter and the receiver parameter of its `when`, `at` or `after` trigger (SysML v2 §8.4 Table 33, `checkAcceptActionUsageReceiverBindingConnector`), with its two ends and the chain through the trigger invocation, and a trigger invocation specializes the `Triggers` function of its kind (`checkTriggerInvocationExpressionSpecialization`), its parameters redefining that function's. One mapping of a trigger kind to its function serves the semantic model and the metamodel evaluator. The derived `name` and `type` of a trigger's argument parameter are in consequence those of the parameter it redefines (`condition`, `timeInstant`, `delay`) in the symbol-backed element properties. The held elements are read through the semantic model only: no name resolves to them, and element ids and the default export are unchanged.
