- **The parser now enforces the declaration shape of relationship clauses on classifiers and features.** A classifier declaration admits a single `:>`/`specializes` list ahead of its `disjoint from`/`unions`/`intersects`/`differences` clauses and none of the feature clauses (`:`, `subsets`, `:>>`, `::>`, `=>`, `chains`, `inverse of`, `featured by`), so forms like `part def X :> A :> B;` are now rejected with a diagnostic instead of being silently accepted (#615). On features, specializations must precede relationship clauses, `ordered`/`nonunique` may each be stated once, and a KerML classifier's multiplicity must precede its specialization list. KerML-only relationship clauses written in a `.sysml` file are now reported as KerML notation, matching the existing `featured by` warning.
