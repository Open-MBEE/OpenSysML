- Hover and completion documentation show the element's own documentation: the bodies of the `doc` members it owns, in declaration order, then the `//` and `//* ... */` notes written directly above its declaration. The body of a `doc`, `comment` or `rep` no longer leaks to the declaration parsed after it — which made hover, completion and the REPL's `%print` show, or start at, a previous element's prose, across closing braces and into the standard library (hovering `Anything` showed the `Base` package's doc). A bare `/* ... */` above a declaration is an anonymous `Comment` of the enclosing namespace and is no longer shown as the declaration's documentation.
