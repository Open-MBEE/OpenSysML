- The Go client's `RenderView` takes `WithShow(elements...)` and `WithDepth(n)` beside `WithFullPorts()`, and its `RenderedView.Shown` lists the optional notation elements drawn, as the other clients already read them.
- The Rust client's `AnalysisResult.tool_dry_run` is the typed `ToolDryRun` preview a run under `AnalysisOptions.tool_dry_run` answers with, instead of leaving it on the wire message.
- The Python client's `Model.refresh_diagnostics(disable_lint=(), enable_lint=())` and `Connection.diagnostics(model_hash, ...)` ask `GetDiagnostics` under a lint selection, as `sysml -disable-lint`/`-enable-lint` select them.
- The Julia client's `execute_action`/`execute_state` take `advance`, `seed`, `draws` and `clock_step`, `run_analysis` takes `tool_dry_run` and answers a typed `ToolDryRun`, `render_view` takes `depth`, `list_engines(conn; probe=true)` probes each external engine, and `refresh_diagnostics(model; disable_lint, enable_lint)` asks `GetDiagnostics` under a lint selection.
- The MATLAB client's `executeAction`/`executeState` take `'advance'`, `'seed'`, `'draws'` and `'clockStep'`, `runAnalysis` takes `'toolDryRun'` and decodes the preview into `AnalysisResult.toolDryRun`, `renderView` takes `'depth'`, `listEngines(conn, 'probe', true)` probes each external engine, and `refreshDiagnostics(model, 'disableLint', ..., 'enableLint', ...)` asks `GetDiagnostics` under a lint selection.
