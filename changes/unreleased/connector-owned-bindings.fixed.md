- A binding connector an `interface def`, an interface usage body or a `connection def` owns (`bind a.voltage = b.voltage` over the connector's `end` features) now binds the features the connector's ends attach — `interface i : L connect s.src to k.dst` makes `k.dst.voltage` hold `s.src.voltage`, exactly as a `bind s.src.voltage = k.dst.voltage` written by the containing part does — in either direction and through a chain of connectors (#1857).
