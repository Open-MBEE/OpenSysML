- A SysML usage that declares no multiplicity now states its implicit `[1..1]` (SysML v2 §7.6.3) the way the pilot does: a nameless `Multiplicity` it owns through a non-implied `OwningMembership`, last among its owned relationships, and its `Type::multiplicity`. Reflection serves it (`multiplicity`, `ownedMember`, `ownedElement`, `ownedRelationship`, `ownedMembership`; its `qualifiedName` is null), the RDF and API-JSON conversions write it with a stable id (`<usage>_pmultiplicity`), and the API-JSON re-export of a compact interchange states one per eligible usage. A declared range (`[2]`, a `MultiplicityRange`) is served the same way on every reader — the usage's owned member and `multiplicity`, at its clause — so reflection and the conversions agree; its bounds are not yet served as elements. Eligible for the default are attribute, item, part, view, rendering and port usages a type owns through a FeatureMembership; a declared range, a KerML feature, an expression body's parameters, an action, a subject or objective, a variant or enumerated value, a redefinition (whatever it redefines) and a usage subsetting a type-owned feature state none.
