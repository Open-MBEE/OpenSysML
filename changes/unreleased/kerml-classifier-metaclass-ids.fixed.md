- A KerML classifier declaration (`classifier`, `class`, `struct`, `assoc`, `assoc struct`, `behavior`, `predicate`, `interaction`, `datatype`, `function`, `metaclass`) now converts to RDF and the API's JSON element form under its KerML metaclass (`Classifier`, `Class`, `Structure`, `Association`, `AssociationStructure`, …) instead of a placeholder `ClassUsage`, `StructureUsage`, … the metamodel does not declare, and `classifier` no longer converts as a `Class`. Its `specializes` is written as a `Subclassification`, and the identity reader and the reflection queries report the id the conversion writes. **Ids change:** the relationship of such a declaration's `specializes` moves from the `_sp<n>` suffix to `_sc<n>` (and its `-id uuid` with it), as a SysML definition's has. Without source text these declarations convert back in their KerML form (`class Thing;`, `classifier Part { … }`) rather than with a `def`. A `type` declaration converts as a `Type`. Graphs written before still read.
