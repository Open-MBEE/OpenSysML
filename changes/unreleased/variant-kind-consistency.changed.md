- A variation is always abstract (SysML v2 §7.6.7, `validateDefinitionVariationIsAbstract`, `validateUsageVariationIsAbstract`), so api-json and Turtle now state `isAbstract: true` for every variation definition, variation usage and enumeration definition, as they already stated `isVariation`; Turtle read back as notation writes `variation` alone, never `abstract variation`.
- The reflective `featuringType` of a variant of a variation usage is that usage's featuringTypes rather than the usage itself (`checkUsageVariationUsageTypeFeaturing`): a variant is an owned member, not an owned feature, of its variation.
