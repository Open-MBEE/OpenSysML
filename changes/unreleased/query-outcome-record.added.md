- A failed document query carries the QVTS outcome record behind its message: `queryexec.Error.Outcome`, a `pathError` (`Failure{step, expected, frontier}` per failing element) or a `modelError` (`Finding{step, element, constraint, observed}`), field for field with `lean/QVTS/Outcome.lean`. `Named` fills it; `-run-query` prints it under the error line and `-json` writes it as `checks[].outcome`, `%run-query` prints it, and the gRPC service attaches it to the failed `RunDocumentQuery`/`RenderDocument` call as a `QueryOutcome` error detail.
