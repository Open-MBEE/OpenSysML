- `sysml-wasm` and `sysml-engine` serve `RenderDocument`: a browser or stdio consumer runs the whole document pipeline — the definition's queries, its document plan and tree, and the Markdown, HTML or AsciiDoc backend — without a service, taking and answering the protojson of the service's `RenderDocumentRequest` and `RenderDocumentResponse` so a typed client's `renderDocument` works over either transport. The combined module advertises `render_document`, `render_document_html`, `render_document_asciidoc` and `render_document_file_name`; `pdf` is refused naming the three forms, and a diagram block whose view carries layout positions is written as Mermaid with the renderer's Graphviz notice, since no WebAssembly build runs Graphviz. The service's `RenderDocument` and the workspace's Markdown rendering now share one pipeline (`internal/doc/docset`).
