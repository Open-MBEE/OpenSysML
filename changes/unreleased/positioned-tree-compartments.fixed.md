- In a positioned tree rendering (a migrated block definition diagram), a member of a drawn definition or usage that has no `DiagramLayout::Layout` of its own — the attributes, nested parts, constraints, ports and enum literals a source tool listed in compartments and gave no symbol — is drawn as a row of a compartment inside its owner's box instead of being left undrawn: rows are grouped by kind in the graphical notation's compartment order (`attributes`, `parts`, `ports`, `actions`, `constraints`, …), written as the member's textual notation (`gain : dB = 15.0`), kept inside the box's `Layout` size in DOT with the rows there is no room for counted in a `// not represented:` notice, and no edge is written to them. Members with a `Layout` of their own, unpositioned trees and renderings with `showCompartments = false` are unchanged. All of DOT, Mermaid, PlantUML and D2 draw the rows; the text and data forms are unchanged.
