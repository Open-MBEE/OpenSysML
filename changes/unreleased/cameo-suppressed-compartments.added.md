- `sysml -migrate sysml` keeps the compartments a modeler collapsed on a Cameo symbol: the symbol's `SUPPRESS_*` compartment properties, its own and the ones inherited from the diagram's symbol styles, are written as `showAttributes`, `showParts`, `showReferences`, `showPorts`, `showConstraints`, `showItems` and `showActions = false` on the symbol's `DiagramLayout::Layout`, and the migration report counts them (`# compartments suppressed: N on M symbols`). The positioned-tree renderer honours the switches per symbol: a hidden compartment is neither listed in the box nor counted among the rows clipped by its stated size, while a rendering's `showCompartments = false` still hides every compartment.
