- **A worked example of a game as a system.** `examples/lord-demo/` models *Legend of the Red
  Dragon*: the town and its shops as parts, a warrior whose day is a state machine the menu's
  signals drive and whose deeds are the actions its transitions perform, a fight whose dice are
  a decision the schedule resolves — checked over every schedule for the one that kills the
  warrior and replayed from its witness — requirements on who may face the dragon, the solver
  on what a first day affords and the fewest hit points that win the brawl, and a generated
  document of the warrior's standing and both price lists.
