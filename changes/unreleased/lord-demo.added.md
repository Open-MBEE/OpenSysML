- **A worked example of a game as a system.** `examples/lord-demo/` models *Legend of the Red
  Dragon* with every mechanic a warrior meets as executable SysML: the town, both shops, the
  masters, all twelve forest levels of monsters and the Red Dragon as parts; a warrior whose day
  is a state machine the menu's signals drive and whose deeds are the actions its transitions
  perform — the forest, the dragon's lair, the healer, the bank and its vault, the shops, the
  training hall, the three skill guilds, the inn's rooms, bribes, gems, Violet and Seth Able,
  the slaughter of other warriors, the fairies, the Old Hag, the Dark Cloak Tavern and midnight
  — each guarded so the warrior's constraints hold however it is reached; fights whose dice are
  decisions the schedule resolves, checked over every schedule for the one that kills the
  warrior and replayed from its witness; requirements on who may face the dragon, wed or
  slaughter; the solver on what a first day affords, the fewest hit points that win the brawl
  outright and the fewest that win it with power moves; and a generated *Daily Happenings* of
  the warrior's standing and the realm's lists.
