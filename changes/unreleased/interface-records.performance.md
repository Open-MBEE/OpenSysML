- **A closed document can be held as its interface record.** `Workspace.InterfaceRecord` writes, from a document's completed analysis, what other documents can observe of it through the language — every element its qualified names and imports reach, with kind, name, aliases, supertypes, features with type, direction, multiplicity, redefinitions and subsetting, abstract/variation, unit, dimension and metadata facts, the imports and filters of each namespace, and the relationships the OOSEM and MOSA audits gather — beside its diagnostics, and `Workspace.OpenRecorded` installs it as tree-less scopes and symbols with the facts attached. A recorded document is never re-analysed and reports its stored diagnostics verbatim; a question only its syntax tree answers — editing, renaming, listing the references it writes, printing its notation, building a runtime — is refused with a typed `symbols.ErrNeedsHydration` rather than answered from the record. The record is the library cache's `IndexRecord` generalised and lives in the same cache (`libs.Cache`), keyed by document name, content hash, library digest, conformance mode, build and format version; it is installed with the content it was written from, which the document keeps so its stored diagnostics locate in it, and it records the conformance mode those diagnostics answer — a workspace holding a record keeps that mode, and `SetConformanceMode` names the document that must be hydrated to change it. `TestInterfaceRecordDifferential` holds every fixture and OMG corpus document recorded in turn and requires every other document's diagnostics and resolutions to be unchanged. No reader is switched to records yet; `docs/internals/interface-records.md` states what a record carries and why, and `docs/internals/performance.md` what it holds against a loaded plane of the satellite stress model.
