- The relationship edges of the DOT, PlantUML, Mermaid, D2 and VS Code canvas writers now follow the glyphs of the SysML v2 graphical notation (§8.2.3) rather than UML habits: a typing is a solid hollow triangle with two dots at its base (`--:|>`), a redefinition a hollow triangle with a bar (`--||>`), a subsetting a plain hollow triangle with no `subsets` text, an owned membership a circle-plus at the namespace, a flow a solid arrow with a filled head, an action succession a dashed arrow with an open head (the Pilot draws it solid), a state transition solid with an open head, a binding labelled `=`, and satisfy, verify, allocate, include, perform and exhibit solid open arrows labelled `«satisfy»`, `«verify»`, `«allocate»`, `«include»`, `«perform»` and `«exhibit»`; an import reads `«import»`, `«import»*` or `«import»**`. The view model gives subsetting, redefinition, perform and exhibit edge kinds of their own (`subsetting`, `redefinition`, `perform`, `exhibit`), which the extension's `hideRelations` setting accepts. The notation table in `docs/project/view-rendering-forms.md` lists every glyph per form with what each medium approximates, and a test holds the writers to it.
