The fixes cleared the interpreted `Fib(25)` (now 20% faster than 0.9.2), the batch constraint check and the cached gRPC diagnostics, and halved the workspace-edit allocations; what remains is loading 10–16% slower per element on connector-dense models, executing a step 20–60%, workspace edits beside a large document at 2.9–3.0 times the bytes, the import-ambiguity warning firing 134 times on the Annex A vehicle example, and the exact-numeric calcs, each with its cause named.
