- `sysml-mcp` authorizes every tool call by scope: each tool of the catalog requires one of `sysml:read`, `sysml:write`, `sysml:execute` or `sysml:session` (named by `tool_catalog` and the manual page's Tools and Scopes sections), and a `tools/call` by a principal whose credential lacks it is refused with a JSON-RPC error naming the tool and the scope, `tools/list` left whole. REPL sessions belong to the principal that opened them, so two principals' `default` or same-named sessions are two sessions and each lists and closes only its own; `sysml:admin` sees every principal's sessions with their owners through `session_list` and closes another's through `session_close` with `owner`. Over stdin and stdout, and over `-http` without a token, the one local caller holds every scope, so nothing changes for a host that starts the server itself. Every `tools/call` is recorded on the log at level `info` with the principal, its source, the remote address, the tool, its scope, the outcome and the duration, never the credential or the arguments (`docs/reference/sysml-mcp.md` § Scopes).
