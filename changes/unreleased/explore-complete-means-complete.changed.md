- `explore` and `check` no longer report `complete` over a construct whose unordered alternatives the runtime performs in one order without a choice point. Such a construct is a typed coverage gap (`Exploration.Unenumerated`, `CheckReport.Unenumerated`; none is recorded today, the repeated-step interleavings that were one being choice points now): the exploration is incomplete while one stands, its status reads `incomplete after N runs; not enumerated: …`, the result's standing is observed rather than proved, and the gap is carried by identifier in the REPL verdict, the gRPC `ExplorationStatus.unenumerated` field and the Python and Node clients' `unenumerated`. Conformance cases state the gaps exploring them records under `exploreGaps`, replacing the prose `exploreNotes`.
- The fixed scheduling policies (`reverse`, `declared`, `seed:<n>`) record the step order their round of due do behaviors takes ahead of a dispatch that would act as the `step order` choice point the unit-at-a-time policies draw, so a default run reports the same choice points `explore` enumerates; nothing about what runs changes, and the default trace of a state machine with a do behavior beside such a dispatch gains one `choice at t=…` line per such move.
