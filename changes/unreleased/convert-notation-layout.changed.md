- `sysml model.sysml -convert sysml` (and `-convert kerml`, and the notation `-migrate sysml` writes) lays the statements of the notation out: a new line after the `{` of a body and after each `;` in it, the `}` on a line of its own, nesting indented — so a model generated on one line comes back one statement per line. Every lexeme and every line break the author wrote are kept; a body holding only an expression, an empty body, the body of an expression and an end-of-line note stay as written. The editor's Format Document, the MCP `format` tool and a save of textual notation keep the author's line breaks as before.
