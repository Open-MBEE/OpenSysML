- **The Rust client reaches every service RPC through a typed API.** `opensysml` adds `parse_sources` with `SourceDocument`, `convert` with `IdForm`, structured and OSLC `query`, `run_document_query` and `render_document`, action and state execution and exploration, verification with bounds, standings and witnesses, `validate_instance`, `calc`, `run_analysis`, `run_sweep`, `list_engines`, and an `Editor`/`Body` builder over `ApplyEdits` that edits a model of several documents. Every call checks the capabilities it and its argument values need before it is sent, naming the remedy; failures are typed (`Error::Execution` with its `FailureReason`, `Error::WrongKind`, `Error::Edit`, `Error::AnalysisRun` keeping the partial result); `Model` gains `find`, `get`, `lookup` and `contains`, and symbols their type facts. The Rust conformance runner drives every RPC through these calls.
