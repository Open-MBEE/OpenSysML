- The VS Code diagram panel gains the navigation of a GUI diagram tool, all panel-only and writing nothing to the model: Ctrl+wheel or pinch zoom about the pointer, drag-to-pan on empty canvas, **Fit** and **1:1** buttons with `+`/`-`/`0` keys, a viewport each panel keeps across reloads; click-to-select, which emphasises a node's relationships and neighbours and dims the rest; a **Relations** list showing or hiding relationship kinds per panel, with `opensysml.diagram.hideRelations` as a workspace-wide default; and **Auto layout**, which lays the diagram out with ELK ignoring the model's `Layout`/`Route` annotations until the next rendering or **Reset to model**.
