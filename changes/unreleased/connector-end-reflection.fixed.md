- **Reflect connector ends and owned relationships.** A connection usage's `connect` clause now owns the ends it writes, named or not, so `ownedMember`, `ownedElement`, `ownedFeature`, `ownedEndFeature` and `endFeature` of its metaobject hold the two end `ReferenceUsage`s, and the API JSON export lists them under `ownedMember` as it did under `connectorEnd`. `ownedSubsetting`, `ownedRedefinition`, `ownedReferenceSubsetting`, `ownedTyping`, `ownedSpecialization` and their siblings now derive for every element as `Relationship` metaobjects whose `source`, `target`, `redefinedFeature`, `subsettedFeature`, `referencedFeature` and the rest are readable, a target written as a feature chain answering its `chainingFeature`; they used to fail with `reflective feature is not derived`. A usage of an interface whose definition pairs its ends by a `flow` no longer warns that the ends are not conjugate.
