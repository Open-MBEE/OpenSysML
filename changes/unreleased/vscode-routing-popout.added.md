- The VS Code diagram panel's `opensysml.diagram.routing` setting sends every edge the automatic layout did not route through the panel's libavoid router when set to `router`, a stated `DiagramLayout::Route` included: the stated routes stay in the model, undrawn, and their waypoint handles are hidden. `model`, the default, draws a stated route verbatim as before. The setting is the panel's alone; `-render-lines` and the exports keep the stated routes.
- `SysML: Move Diagram to New Window` (in the diagram panel's title-bar menu and the command palette) moves the diagram into a floating window, keeping its drawing, chosen view and style; a diagram opened afterwards joins it there. `opensysml.diagram.openIn: newWindow` moves the first diagram out as soon as it opens.
