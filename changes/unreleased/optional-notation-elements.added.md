- A view's rendering now draws only the notation elements the SysML v2 notation requires plus the port symbols, compartments and keywords, and names the optional ones to show, each on its own: `parameter-symbols` (a case's actor, subject and stakeholder parameters and an action's parameter pins on the node's boundary), `port-symbols`, `compartments`, `edge-labels`, `keywords`, `multiplicities`, `typings` and `qualifiers`, or `all`. A rendering specializing a standard `Views` rendering chooses them in the model with Boolean `showParameterSymbols`, `showEdgeLabels`, … attributes, the nearest declaration deciding each; `sysml -render-show <elements>` on `-render`, `-render-all`, `-render-document` and `-render-documents`, `show` on the LSP `opensysml/render` request (the elements listed by the `openSysmlRenderElements` capability), `show=<elements>` on the REPL's `%render` and `%render-document`, `show` on `opensysml/renderDocument`, on the sysml-engine `RenderView` request and on the gRPC `RenderView` RPC (their results' `shown` listing what was drawn; `model.render_view(view, show=[...])` in Python, `show` on the Node, Java, Julia and MATLAB `renderView`/`render_view`, `Model::render_view_showing` in Rust and `WithShow` in Go), the VS Code `opensysml.diagram.show` setting and a document diagram block's `show` attribute (`attribute redefines show = "edge-labels, typings";`) add to what the rendering shows. A connection that ended at a parameter symbol or port left out ends at the node that declares it, and a label left out leaves no empty box.
