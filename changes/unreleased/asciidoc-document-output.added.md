- `-doc-form asciidoc` renders a document as AsciiDoc from the compiled document tree — `=`/`==` headings by level, block anchors and `<<anchor,text>>` cross references, `link:` macros, `_emphasis_`, `*strong*`, `` `code` `` and `stem:[…]` runs under a `:stem: latexmath` header, `|===` tables with `[cols]` percentages from `columnWidths` and spanning group rows, `*`/`.` lists, `term::` definitions, `[stem]` formula blocks, `image::` macros and diagram sources in `[source,<form>]` listings, every model string escaped so it cannot open AsciiDoc structure — and `-render-documents -doc-form asciidoc` writes a set of `.adoc` files whose cross-document references are `xref:` macros. The gRPC and Connect `RenderDocument` call takes `form: "asciidoc"` and answers an `asciidoc` field under the new `render_document_asciidoc` capability, and the Java, Python, Node, Rust, Julia and MATLAB clients accept the form.
