- `feature x chains a, b;` and `feature x inverse of a, b;` are syntax errors at the comma: a `chains` clause takes one feature chain (`chains a.b`) and an `inverse of` clause one feature, where `featured by` and the specialization clauses list their targets (KerML 1.1 8.2.4.3.1 ChainingPart, InvertingPart; 8.2.4.3.5 FeatureChain; 8.2.4.3.6 OwnedFeatureInverting). The surplus targets were read as further chaining or inverted features without a diagnostic.
