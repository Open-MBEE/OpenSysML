- The RDF conversion states a Membership's ends and names as KerML derives them: `source` is the owning namespace, `target` the member, `relatedElement` both in that order, and `memberName` / `memberShortName` the member's effective names (KerML 8.3.2.3.5) — an unnamed feature is named by the one it redefines, so `in item;` of `shoot : Shoot` is the member `image`, on the reflective read as in the graph. Every membership shape — OwningMembership, FeatureMembership, FeatureValue, SubjectMembership, ParameterMembership, ResultExpressionMembership — carries them, as the reflective read already answered them. Reading a graph back treats them as the restatement of the ownership edges they are.
