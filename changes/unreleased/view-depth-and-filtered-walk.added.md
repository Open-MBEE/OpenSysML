- A view's filter conditions — its `filter` members, the filter clauses of its `expose` lines and those inherited from its view definition — now prune what a tree or interconnection rendering draws beneath each exposed element: a member a condition rejects is not drawn, nor its subtree, so `filter @SysML::PartUsage;` draws the parts without their attributes and ports and `filter not @Hidden;` blacklists the elements marked `#Hidden`. A connector whose end is pruned is counted in the notices instead of drawn. A view with no filter conditions renders as before.
- An attribute usage `depth` on a view (`view v { expose vehicle; attribute depth = 2; render asTreeDiagram; }`), or on the nearest view definition it specializes, bounds how many levels of nested elements a tree or interconnection rendering draws below each exposed element; `0` or none draws every level. An element at the bound keeps its ports and compartment rows and carries the note `nested deeper than N levels; not shown`, and the elements directly below the bound are counted once in the notices. A view definition may declare the attribute typed (`attribute depth : ScalarValues::Natural[0..1];`) for its specializations to redefine; a `depth` bound to anything but a non-negative integer literal is the `view-depth-value` error.
- `sysml -render-depth <levels>` (with `-render` and `-render-all`), `%render … depth=<levels>`, the LSP `opensysml/render` request's `depth` (advertised as `openSysmlRenderDepth`), the gRPC `RenderViewRequest.depth` — taken by the Python, Node, Rust, Java, Go (`WithDepth`), Julia and MATLAB clients and the sysml-engine `RenderView` request — and the VS Code `opensysml.diagram.depth` setting override the view's depth for one render.
- A `DiagramLayout::Layout` with `collapsed = true` about an element now draws it with no children in every form (text, DOT, Mermaid, PlantUML, D2), keeping its ports and the `collapsed` tag; the text form omits the compartments a Layout's `showParts = false` and the other `show…` switches hide, as DOT already did.
