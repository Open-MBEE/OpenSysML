- Strict conformance (`-strict`, the REPL's `%strict`, the editor's `strictConformance`, the service's `strict_conformance`) now reports a namespace whose memberships are indistinguishable as an error: `Duplicate of other owned member name`, `Duplicate of owned member name`, `Duplicate of other alias name`, the short-name variant and `Duplicate of inherited member name` (KerML `validateNamespaceDistinguishibility`), so `sysml -validate -strict` exits non-zero on `part def A; part def A;`. The default mode keeps them warnings, and `Duplicate of imported member name` stays a warning in every mode, since KerML 7.2.5.4 hides the colliding names rather than leaving the namespace ill-formed.
