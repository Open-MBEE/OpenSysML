- CI bounds every `actions/checkout` step of the pull-request, merge-queue, action-validation and rebaseline workflows (3 minutes for a shallow checkout that takes about 10 seconds, 10 minutes for a full-history one that takes about 30) and checks out once more when the first attempt fails or is cut off. A fetch that stalls hangs instead of failing, so the action's own three attempts never see it: twice in one day the full-history checkout of the cross-MDK conformance job sat until the job's 30-minute limit cancelled it and the merge-queue group with it.
