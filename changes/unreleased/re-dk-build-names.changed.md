- **The two `sysml` builds are named after the two halves of the Runtime Environment and Development Kit.** The default build with every feature group is the DK (Development Kit) build; the build without the optional and developer-only groups, formerly the "production" build, is the RE (Runtime Environment) build: `-tags sysml_re` replaces `-tags sysml_prod`, `make build-re` and `make build-wasm-re` replace `make build-prod` and `make build-wasm-prod`, and they write `bin/re/sysml` and `bin/wasm/{js,wasip1}/re/sysml.wasm`. The binary is named `sysml` in both builds; RE and DK are names of the packages, not of the executables. What each build links is unchanged, as are the per-group `sysml_no<group>` tags.
