- The reflective read of an expression now owns what the KerML metamodel says it owns, as the RDF conversion already wrote it: one `in` parameter per operand (and one per type argument), each held by a `ParameterMembership` and binding its operand through a `FeatureValue`, and the `out` result parameter of an operator, index, reference, chain, collect or select expression, held by a `ReturnParameterMembership`. `argument`, `parameter`, `input`, `output`, `result`, `ownedFeature`, `ownedMembership` and `ownedElement` of a value expression answer them, nested operands included, and their `elementId`s are the ones the conversion writes. An invocation's and a body expression's own parameters are not yet synthesized.
