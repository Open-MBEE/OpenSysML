- **Two lints: `undeclared-signal` and `port-type-mismatch`.** A `when <name>` or `defer <name>` whose name matches no declaration in scope and no signal the model sends is reported with did-you-mean suggestions, and a `connect`, interface usage or `flow` between ports whose definitions are unrelated and not conjugate-compatible is reported, where both were accepted silently. Both are warnings in every mode, `-strict` included; switch one off with `-disable-lint <code>`, `%lint <code> off` or the `disabledLints` editor setting.
