- `explore` no longer copies a run's choice record once per choice it leaves unexplored when it queues the next prefixes (quadratic in the run's choice points); the queue shares the record and builds a prefix's plan when its run starts. Which linearizations are run, their order and the budgets reported do not change; `explore:runs=6,depth=2000` on a ring of nine state machines takes about 40% less CPU.
