- **The execution runtime computes a statement's order and stamped footprint once per model, not per executor or pass.** An assignment in an action body records what it writes through the statement's footprint with the performance's own places stamped as its; that stamping cloned the statement's read and write places on every execution, two allocations per executed statement, so a `for` loop of a thousand passes allocated two thousand more objects than before. The statement order the footprint comes from was also memoized per executor, so a chain of a thousand actions rebuilt every body's order on every run, and a state's entry or do behavior rebuilt its order on every entry. Both are now computed once per model, and a footprint with nothing to stamp is used as it is.
