- **The Rust client replaces a private sysml-grpc child that dies as it is joined.** A killed child closes its sockets a moment before its exit can be observed, so `Connection::private()` could join the dying child and fail its handshake with a connection reset or refusal. A joined child whose handshake fails is now given a moment to report its exit and, once it has, is replaced; one that stays alive still surfaces the handshake error.
