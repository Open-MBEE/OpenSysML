- **Route a moved node's edges at right angles around the other boxes in the diagram panel.** An edge at a node the model places was drawn as a straight line; the panel now routes it orthogonally around the other boxes with libavoid whenever it lays the canvas out, separating edges that share a box and keeping it straight only until the router has loaded, during a drag, or when no route fits. The route is not written to the model unless one of its waypoints is edited.
- **Bundle libavoid in the diagram panel.** The panel ships libavoid 0.4.5 (LGPL-2.1-or-later) as WebAssembly and loads it at startup; until it answers, edges it would route stay straight.
