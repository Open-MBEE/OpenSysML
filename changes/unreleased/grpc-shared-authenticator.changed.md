- `sysml-grpc` verifies a call's credential through the authenticator chain `sysml-mcp` verifies through, built by one shared reading of `-token-file`, `-token` and `OPENSYSML_GRPC_TOKEN`, so the two commands cannot drift; the principal a call is admitted as is carried in its context for the service to read, the access log names its `subject` and `source`, and a credential lacking a scope the service requires is answered `permission_denied` (HTTP 403, `error="insufficient_scope"`) and one that cannot be verified for now `unavailable` (HTTP 503). Every other refusal, status, the loopback rule and `-allow-insecure-token-transport` are unchanged.
