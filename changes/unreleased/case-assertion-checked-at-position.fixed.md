- An `assert constraint` that a succession orders among the steps of an analysis or verification case is now judged where the flow reaches it, against the values the case holds at that point, rather than after the whole body: a later step reassigning a local the assertion reads no longer changes its verdict. In the case a run reports on the flow moves on whatever the verdict, while a violated assertion of a case performed as a step of another still ends the run; an ordered assertion the flow never reaches is reported `undecided`, and one reached more than once reports its first violation. An assertion no succession orders is still judged once the body has completed. An assertion typed by a constraint definition reads the inputs it binds (`in v = level;`) at its position rather than the definition's defaults, and an asserted constraint that states no condition is reported `undecided` instead of being left out of the verdicts.
