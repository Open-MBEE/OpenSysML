- An `assert constraint` that a succession orders among the steps of an analysis or verification case is now judged where the flow reaches it, against the values the case holds at that point, rather than after the whole body: a later step reassigning a local the assertion reads no longer changes its verdict. The flow moves on whatever the verdict, an ordered assertion the flow never reaches is reported `undecided`, and one reached more than once reports its first violation. An assertion no succession orders is still judged once the body has completed.
