- `-self-check-package` house rules (and `meta` casts) can read the relationship-valued features of the metamodel: `Element::ownedRelationship`, `ownedAnnotation`, `owningRelationship`, `owningMembership`, `textualRepresentation`; `Namespace::ownedMembership`, `ownedImport`; `Type::ownedFeatureMembership`, `featureMembership`, `inheritedMembership`; `AnnotatingElement::annotation`, `annotatedElement`, `ownedAnnotatingRelationship`, `owningAnnotatingRelationship`, `Documentation::documentedElement`; and the requirement properties their memberships derive — `text`, `requiredConstraint`, `assumedConstraint`, `framedConcern`, `actorParameter`, `stakeholderParameter`, `VerificationCase*::verifiedRequirement`, `satisfiedRequirement`, `assertedConstraint`, `satisfyingFeature`. Memberships, Annotations and Imports reflect as metaobjects of their own (`OwningMembership`, `FeatureMembership`, `RequirementConstraintMembership` with `kind`, `RequirementVerificationMembership`, `SubjectMembership`, `Annotation`, `NamespaceImport`, …) with the ends and attributes their metaclass declares, and `disjoint from`, `unions`, `intersects`, `differences`, `featured by` and `inverse of` clauses reflect as `Disjoining`, `Unioning`, `Intersecting`, `Differencing`, `TypeFeaturing` and `FeatureInverting`. These rules were reported as "reflective feature is not derived" (unevaluated) before.
