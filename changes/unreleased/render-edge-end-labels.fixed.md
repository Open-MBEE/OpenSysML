- **An association end's name is drawn at the end it names, clear of the other labels.** A composite part's name and multiplicity on the composition to its type, a `ref`'s on its reference edge, and each end of a connection def drawn as a line between its end types, are the name of the edge's end (`Edge.FromLabel`, `Edge.ToLabel`), not the edge's own text: the DOT form sets it as `headlabel`, placed by `head_lp` beside the last segment of a routed edge before that end, PlantUML in quotes before the end's alias and D2 as the arrowhead's label; Mermaid's flowchart and the text form, which have no place at an end, keep it in the edge's text. A routed view's labels are placed in order, each clear of the drawn boxes, the notes, every routed line, the canvas's edge and the labels before it — the other side of the line, then a step along it — so compositions whose Cameo routes share a trunk no longer stack their names at one midpoint.
