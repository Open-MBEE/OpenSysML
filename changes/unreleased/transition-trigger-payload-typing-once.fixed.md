- A transition whose trigger accepts a typed payload (`transition t first s1 accept Sig then s2;`, `accept x : Sig`) no longer reflects the payload's `FeatureTyping` more than once under one `elementId`. The trigger's `AcceptActionUsage` read the payload's head clauses as its own and answered the payload's id, and the transition owned a named payload through a membership of its own beside the accept's `ParameterMembership`, so a query over the model's relationships returned two or three rows carrying `<payload>_ft0`. The typing is now owned by the payload parameter alone, as for the same `accept` written as an action, and the accept reads its own id (`<transition>::@trigger`).
