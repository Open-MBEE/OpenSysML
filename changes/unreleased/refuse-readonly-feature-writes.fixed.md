- **A behavior no longer writes a `constant` or `derived` feature.** An assignment (direct, qualified or through a feature chain), a flow or succession flow into a feature, an accepted payload bound into a feature the behavior declares, an entry/do/exit or transition effect, a performer's write and a session's `SetFeatureValue` now refuse such a target — and one redefining or subsetting a constant feature, or redefining a derived one — with `runtime.ErrReadOnlyFeature`, leaving the value as it was and naming the feature and whether it is constant or derived. A constant still takes its initial value or default, and a derived feature is still bound and re-evaluated. The constraint tier reports the same assignments as `assignment-referent-read-only` wherever the target, or a chain's final feature, resolves statically.
