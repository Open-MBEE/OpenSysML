- **The fUML referee's emitter translates classes, objects and signals.** A fUML `Class` with attributes and generalizations becomes a `part def`, `CreateObjectAction` a `new` occurrence on its result pin, `ReadSelfAction` `this`, and the structural feature actions (`Read`, `Add`, `Remove`, `Clear`) feature reads and assignments on the object at the `object` pin, positioned as the reference implementation positions them; a `Signal` becomes an `attribute def` specializing its generals, `SendSignalAction` a `send new <Signal>(…) to target` and `AcceptEventAction` an `accept` node whose result pin is the instance received, an instance of a specialized signal satisfying an accept of its general. The referee gives class-typed inputs defaulted objects and compares object outputs by class and feature values; five activities of the reference suite move from `not-expressible` to `pass` (20 pass, 0 fail, 31 not-expressible, 4 differs-by-design).
- **An accepted signal flows on from the accept node's result pin.** The action-graph lowering records an `accept <name> : <Signal>` node's payload as an output feature, and the executor binds the value received on the accept's own performance as well as in the enclosing body, so `flow receiver.msg to consumer.value` carries it to the consumer and `value.level` reads its attributes.
