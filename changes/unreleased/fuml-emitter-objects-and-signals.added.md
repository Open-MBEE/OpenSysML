- **The fUML referee translates classes, object creation and the structural feature actions.** `tools/cmd/fuml-referee` spells a fUML `Class` as a `part def` with its generalizations and its attributes at their declared multiplicity, `CreateObjectAction` as `new <Class>()`, `ReadSelfAction` as `this`, and `Read`/`Add`/`Remove`/`ClearStructuralFeatureAction` as reads of and `assign`ments to the object's feature that leave what the reference implementation computes — replacing, first-position, indexed and appending adds, unique features keeping one copy, first-copy, indexed and every-copy removes. Activities take class-typed inputs defaulted as the reference defaults them and return objects compared by class and feature values. Five activities move from `not-expressible` to `pass` (20 of 55), each adjudicated in `docs/project/fuml-referee.md`.
