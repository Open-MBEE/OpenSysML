- A `concern def`, `viewpoint def`, requirement or case whose first owned member is a `stakeholder` or `actor` and that declares no `subject` is now reported with `Subject must be first parameter.` (SysML `validateRequirementDefinitionSubjectParameterPosition`): an actor or stakeholder is an `in` parameter, so it displaces the subject the declaration inherits from first position; a `frame concern` of such a definition is reported too, an owned subject declared after one carries the error itself, and a declaration whose subject (named or anonymous `subject;`) comes first stays silent.
