- Classifying a due-order choice among executors whose bodies read and write through parts no longer rescans every statement of a move for each write of another that meets one of its reads: each move's statements are indexed once per draw, shared among alternatives with the same footprint, and the places two moves may name one value through are decided once per pair, without a slice or closure per test. What a choice point touches is unchanged; `explore` over such models runs about twice as fast as in 0.12.0.
