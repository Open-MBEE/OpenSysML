- `Real` is now the extended reals of KerML §9.3.2.2.9: `-*` is the negative infinity, below every number, an `Integer`, a `Rational` and a `Real` but no `Natural`, and arithmetic over an infinity answers what the extended reals determine (`* + 1` is `*`, `1 - *` is `-*`, `2 / *` is the Rational `0.0`, `(-*) ** 3` is `-*`, `sqrt(*)` is `*`, `exp(-*)` is `0.0`) where it was an error. An indeterminate form (`* - *`, `0 * *`, `* / *`, `* ** 0`, any `%`) is an arithmetic domain error, and a finite result too large for binary64 is still an overflow. On the wire `-*` is the `real_value` `-Infinity`, and a non-finite `real_value` sent in reads as the infinity it denotes.
