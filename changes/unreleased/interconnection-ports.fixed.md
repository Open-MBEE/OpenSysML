- The interconnection rendering now draws the ports a part has from its definition — `part heating : HeatingSystem` shows the `durationIn : ~DurationPort` its definition declares — on the part's border, named and typed as declared with `~` for a conjugated one, and ends a connector, interface, flow or binding at the port it names rather than at the part: DOT as record cells or pin nodes (`"n2":"n2.0" -> "n1":"n1.0"`), PlantUML as `port` elements of the part's rectangle, text as `port` lines under the part and `part.port` edge ends, Mermaid as a `subgraph` per ported part holding a node per pin (`n1.0["durationIn : ~DurationPort<br>«port»"]`) with the connector drawn pin to pin. Previously only a part def's own nested ports were drawn, so `-render '#interconnection:Toaster'` and a declared interconnection view named no port at all. A part whose definition declares no port draws as before. `-render -help` now states the `#<kind>` and `#<kind>:<element>` pseudo-view forms.
