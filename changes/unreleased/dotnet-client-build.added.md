- The build of a C#/.NET client under `client/dotnet/`, consumed from a checkout by `ProjectReference` rather than published to NuGet: a `net10.0` library, tests and sample, with the SDK pinned in the repository-root `global.json`, centrally pinned packages restored from lock files, analyzers and public-API tracking as build errors, and `make dotnet-test`. The client is experimental: its assemblies are marked `[Experimental("OSML0001")]`, which a project suppresses to use them, and a release may change their API.
