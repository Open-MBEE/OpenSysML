- Automatically laid-out tree diagrams fold a node's owned members into compartment rows of its box on request: `%viz --style=COMPMOST` folds every owned usage no `Layout`, nested definition or package keeps a node, `--style=COMPTREE` the port usages with the ports nested under them, so the pilot's two spellings now draw instead of being noticed as not represented. The folding is `-render-compartments positioned|most|ports` at the CLI (on `-render`, `-render-all` and the document diagrams), `compartments=<folding>` on `%render`, `compartments` on the LSP `opensysml/render` request (listed by the `openSysmlRenderCompartments` capability) and on gRPC `RenderView`, whose rows carry the `compartment` of their parent they are drawn in; the Python, Go, Rust and Node clients take it. An edge at a folded member moves to its owner, the owner's containment of it is dropped as the nesting the row stands for, and the notice counts the members drawn as rows. The default output is unchanged.
