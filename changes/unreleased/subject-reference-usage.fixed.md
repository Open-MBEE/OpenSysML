- A requirement's or case's `subject` is the `ReferenceUsage` its `SubjectMembership` declares (SysML.xtext `SubjectMember`), not a `PartUsage`, on reflection and the query leg alike — as the RDF and API-JSON writers and the pilot already state it. A subject, actor or stakeholder written without a direction is an `in` parameter that is referential (`isComposite` false), in a case body as in a requirement's, on source and recorded models alike. A requirement body's `subject s : T;` now carries its type in the symbol's `type_info` and `metadata`, as a case body's subject does. The parameters a behavior inherits from the library — the `result` of a case or requirement, the `verdict` of a verification case — are listed among its members like a declared `return r`, instead of being withheld with the library members; a declared `return` still masks the inherited parameter it redefines.
