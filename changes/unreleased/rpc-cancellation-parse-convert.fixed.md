- A cancelled or timed-out `ParseFile`, `ParseSources` or `Convert` of a model of several documents now stops working instead of running to its end with the answer withheld: the parse stops before its next document or analysis pass and caches nothing, the conversion before its next document and between batches of elements, and the call fails with `CANCELLED` or `DEADLINE_EXCEEDED`. `ImportData` and `RecordRun` stop the same way while they re-parse the edited model. The other long calls (`Query`, `Migrate`, a single-document `Convert`, the render and export calls) still run to completion.
