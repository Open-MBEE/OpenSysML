- A package-level `action` or `state` usage now denotes one representative performance, as a package-level `part` or `connection` does: a bare read of `P::a` is that object rather than `<undetermined>`, `all Act` counts it beside the nested performances, a stated `[0..*]` denotes none and a fixed `[2]` two. Reading it materializes the performance and starts what its type exhibits or performs, as reading a nested one already did; the action's own body is not run, and `send … to P::a` keeps reaching whatever performs the usage.
