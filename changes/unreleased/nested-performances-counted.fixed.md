- A nested `action` or `state` usage of definite multiplicity declaring no type (`action a1;`, `state s1;`) is a performance object its owner holds before anything performs it, as a typed one already was: `p3.a1` reads the object instead of `<undetermined>`, and the usages count in the Kernel and Systems collections the library says they belong to — `ownedActions`, `ownedStates`, `ownedPerformances`, `involvingPerformances`, `suboccurrences` and `timeEnclosedOccurrences` of a part, `subperformances` and `enclosedPerformances` of an action — as listable sequences (`#(i)`, iteration, `includes`), at model level as on an instantiated object. The context feature `this` of a performance held under `ownedPerformances` is its owner, and of one held under `subperformances` its owner's `this` (Objects.kerml, Performances.kerml), instead of the performance itself.
