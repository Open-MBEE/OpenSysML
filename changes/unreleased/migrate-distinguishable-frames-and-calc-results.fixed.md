- **A migrated viewpoint's framed concerns are distinguishable.** A concern a viewpoint frames by reference, `frame <concern>;`, takes the referenced concern's name as its own, so two framed concerns of one name, or a framed concern named like a stakeholder of the viewpoint, left the viewpoint with indistinguishable memberships (KerML `validateNamespaceDistinguishibility`). Such a frame is now written in the grammar's named form, `frame concern <name> ::> <concern>;`, under a name fresh in the viewpoint and listed in its `SynthesizedName` marker; a frame whose name is its own alone stays `frame <concern>;`.
- **A call of a calc declares its result pin once.** The action written for a `CallBehaviorAction` of a `FunctionBehavior` declared its result pin bare and then again with the call's value, two members named `result`; it now declares the pin once, taking the value.
