- Validating a requirement that `require`s many sibling requirements, or a verification objective that `verify`s them, took time exponential in the member count: each `require`/`verify` member takes its short name from the requirement it references, and resolving that reference asked the owning scope for every sibling's derived short name again, so a group of ten took seconds and twelve never finished. The resolver now memoizes a feature's derived short name once its naming feature is settled, holding one that was asked while a sibling's naming was under way until that sibling proves to have no short name, so the group validates in time linear in its members; a check-level test over a fourteen-member group and objective and a benchmark pin the complexity.
