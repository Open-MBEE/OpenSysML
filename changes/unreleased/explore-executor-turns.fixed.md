- **Executors due at one instant interleave move by move under `explore`, `check`, `replay` and seeded schedules.** An executor drawn from the due order used to run all its work at the instant before another could move, so two actions reading a shared feature and then writing it never reached the lost update. The executor drawn now makes one move and keeps its turn only while its next move is independent of everything each other due executor may still do at the instant; otherwise the due order is drawn again. Explore runs and check state counts grow on models whose messages cannot be resolved statically. `declared` and `reverse` keep their order.
