- `RunRuns`, `SelfCheck` and `RecordRun` RPCs with the `run_runs`, `self_check` and `record_run` MCP tools: repeated action runs and Monte Carlo samples with their observed features tabled and distributed (`sysml -runs`, `-observe`), the reflective self-check of a model against the `SysMLValidation` rule constraints with its findings and counts (`-self-check`, `-self-check-package`), and the recording of a case's run, sweep or sample into the model as a new model the service holds (`-record-run`, `-record-into`). The service advertises the `runs`, `self_check` and `record_run` capabilities; the Go, Python, Node, Java, Rust, Julia and MATLAB clients expose them as `RunAction`/`RunMonteCarlo`/`run_runs`, `SelfCheck`/`self_check` and `RecordRun`/`record_run`.
