- **A view whose only `Layout` is about a performer draws its whole flow again.** Placing a swimlane, which is what dragging a lane in the VS Code diagram panel saves in a view with no other `Layout`, made `sysml -render` leave every other node undrawn in the dot, mermaid, plantuml, d2 and drawio forms (`9 node(s) without a position, left undrawn`), while the panel went on drawing them. A swimlane frames the nodes of its performer, so swimlanes placed alone no longer count as positioning the drawing: the flow is laid out and drawn whole, the draw.io form draws the lane where its `Layout` says, at the stated size when it states one, with the flow drawn around it, and the forms that pin no position keep the `Layout` as a comment. A view that also places a node is drawn as before. Importing a draw.io file in which a swimlane was moved, in a view placing nothing else, now places every flow the file shows rather than the moved lane's flow alone, so the next render leaves no other flow undrawn. The same holds when a node is moved in a view whose only `Layout`s are about performers, and the placed lanes stay where their `Layout`s say. A step with no name is the exception: only a `Layout` on its declaration could place it, in every view, so the import places the rest of its flow, leaves the step the file did not move unplaced, and says so instead of changing other views. A node the view shows twice keeps the place the file moved it to. Flows drawn around lanes placed one under the other put their remaining steps beside the lanes when under them they would cover the other flow's lane. When two such lanes are stated nearer than the other flow's frame needs, the render says so on stderr in every form, and so does an import of the draw.io file, which keeps the notice as a comment: it names both lanes, the gap that keeps them and their flows' frames clear, and the lane, steps and frame drawn over with the size covered. A later render goes on saying it for as long as the frame is drawn over the lane, whatever else an import has placed since. When a `Layout` places the covering frame itself, the notice gives how far that frame and what it holds must be placed to clear everything the other flow draws, a swimlane placed past its own flow's frame included, and lanes stated over each other or against each other are reported as that. A flow no `Layout` places is drawn under everything a placed flow draws, a swimlane placed past that flow's frame included, instead of over that swimlane.
