- **A view whose only `Layout` is about a performer draws its whole flow again.** Placing a swimlane, which is what dragging a lane in the VS Code diagram panel saves in a view with no other `Layout`, made `sysml -render` leave every other node undrawn in the dot, mermaid, plantuml, d2 and drawio forms (`9 node(s) without a position, left undrawn`), while the panel went on drawing them. A swimlane frames the nodes of its performer, so swimlanes placed alone no longer count as positioning the drawing: the flow is laid out and drawn whole, the draw.io form keeps the lane's stated size, and the forms that pin no position keep the `Layout` as a comment. A view that also places a node is drawn as before.
