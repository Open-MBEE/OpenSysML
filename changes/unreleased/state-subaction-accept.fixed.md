- A state's `entry`, `do` and `exit` actions take an accept directly — `entry accept a : Sig via p;`, with a named or unnamed payload, `via`, and with or without a body (SysML v2 §8.2.2.18.1 `StateAcceptActionUsage`) — as the pilot does, where the parser reported `expected an action, an action reference or '{' after 'entry'` and a stray `expected the target of the transition after 'then'`. It is the `AcceptActionUsage` the `action accept …` spelling already was, so the symbol table, queries, the LSP, API JSON and Turtle see one under the `StateSubactionMembership`, where the export wrote a `PerformActionUsage` for either spelling. At run time an entry behavior runs at the instant of entry up to where its flow waits for a message; the state's entry is then held — its regions, entry transitions, completion, timers and own transitions wait — and goes on in the do rounds when the occurrence arrives, the do behavior and the completion following it, where the runtime ran such an entry as an action of no content.
