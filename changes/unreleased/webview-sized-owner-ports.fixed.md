- The VS Code diagram panel keeps a `Layout` with a stated `width` and `height` as a fixed box: the `port` usages under it are drawn as pins on its border, each connector anchored at its pin, and an unplaced member the box has no room for is left undrawn with a notice, as a stated size leaves compartment rows unwritten in the rendered forms. Ports of an unsized owner are pinned on its border the same way, the box fitted to its other members; a port the model places keeps its stated box, and one with members drawn under it stays a box inside its owner. Before, a 60 px box laid its ports out as 52 px boxes inside it, below its bottom edge.
