- OSLC Query text follows the specification's grammar where it departed from it: a scoped sort term carries its signs inside the braces (`oslc.orderBy=sysml:owner{+sysml:name,-sysml:qualifiedName}`, the percent-encoded form a stock OSLC client sends), `in` may be followed directly by its list (`sysml:name in["a","b"]`), a `<uri>` value decodes the `\>` and `\\` escapes of `uri_ref_esc`, a `"…"^^rdf:XMLLiteral` literal is read as a plain string, and `oslc.paging` and `oslc.pageSize` are accepted and ignored rather than refused as unknown. A decimal is the lexical form of `xsd:decimal`, so `inf`, `NaN` and `1e3` are refused as values instead of reaching the evaluator, where `sysx:multiplicityUpper<inf` failed with an untyped error; the structured API Query refuses them the same way. The reference documents the string-comparison semantics (case-sensitive, whole value, no `%`/`_` patterns) and that `oslc.searchTerms` computes no relevance score.
