- A query now reads the ends of the relationships it used to refuse with "a metamodel property OpenSysML does not derive yet". `Relationship::source`, `target` and `relatedElement` answer on a connector (`binding`, `succession`, `connector`, in KerML and SysML) as the features its ends reference, and on a `dependency` as its clients and suppliers. A keyword-first `disjoining`, `inverting` or `featuring` answers `typeDisjoined`/`disjoiningType`, `featureInverted`/`invertingFeature` and `featureOfType`/`featuringType` in the order the notation writes them, and the `source` and `target` they redefine. `Expression::operator` reads `.` on a feature chain expression, `collect` on `a.{…}` and `select` on `a.?{…}`. The typing a transition's `accept` trigger writes resolves its `target` whichever element is read first.
