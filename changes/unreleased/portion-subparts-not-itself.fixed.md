- Reading `subparts`, `subitems` or `suboccurrences` of a `timeslice` or `snapshot` part nested in a part no longer fails with `occurrence lifetime does not admit the change: … as a portion of itself`. A portion reads the whole's parts as its own, but the whole's portions — the portion itself and its sibling slices — are no parts of it, so they are left out and the portion's list is empty where the whole holds nothing else (`cruise.subparts = []` under `part def P { timeslice part cruise; }`, as `%features` of `examples/relay-probe-demo` shows under `relay.cruise`).
